
# Visit schedule conventions (Larry's case-management caseload)

- **QV (Quarterly Visit): due BY THE END OF THE MONTH.** Phoenix shows the month-end date (e.g. 10/31/2026) —
  that is the real due date, not a placeholder. (Larry confirmed 2026-10-06.)
- **REV (Re-Evaluation): has an EXACT due date** (e.g. 10/15, 10/16).
- **MC (Monthly Contact): phone contact**, separate from in-home visits — not counted in "visits."
- Monthly visit list artifact: `PHC_October_2026_Visits.md` (workspace root), pulled from Phoenix.
