### What's ALREADY carved into MCOs (effective Jan 1, 2026)
Members moved to managed care for **medical services** (18+, dually-enrolled Medicare+Medicaid,
HIV/AIDS waiver, Vent waiver, Community Choices (CC) waiver, nursing facility residents):
- Behavioral health, pharmacy, DME, home health, hospital, physician, therapy services
- **Incontinence supplies (IS)** — referrals, authorizations, coordination now handled by MCOs.
  Case managers terminate existing IS authorizations in Phoenix; no new IS apps in Phoenix.
- Claims for medical services → member's MCO. Waiver services stay FFS.

### What REMAINS FFS (as of the published docs)
- **Waiver Case Management is explicitly listed as a FFS waiver service** (comparison chart).
- Personal care, companion, adult day health, respite, environmental mods, etc. stay FFS.
- So the *published* documents do NOT yet show case management itself moving to MCOs —
  but the internal confirmation above says it's coming anyway (they knew before IS carve-in).

### MCOs involved (SC Healthy Connections managed care)
- Absolute Total Care (ATC/Centene), Healthy Blue (BlueChoice SC), Select Health (First Choice),
  Molina, Humana (CenterWell).

### FAQ highlights (scdhhs carve-in FAQ page)
- 180-day continuity of care period for newly enrolled MCO members; providers continue delivering.
- No changes to authorization/payment of waiver services (as of FAQ date).
- Prime/Dual (MMP) program sunset Dec 31, 2025 → transitioned to D-SNP Medicare Advantage plans.
