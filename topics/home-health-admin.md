## 2. Authorizations & terminations (Home Care)

- You can print **terminations first** or the **big ones** first — order is preference. (Trainer is "a terminations person.")
- **Why 4 items for 1 client:** a client with **2 services** (companion + personal care) = **2 cancels + 2 restarts** = 4.
- **Workflow:** get the auth number (save off email or the list) → **Terminations → top one → Preview** → it downloads (top-right arrow) → **Cmd/Ctrl+Z paste** → Enter → it pulls them up.
- **Always print on ANY change** — even same authorizations/dates. If an **address or phone** changed → **HIGHLIGHT** the change and file it. Highlight any change so it's obvious later.
- **Accepting an authorization:** check schedule → (companion typically 2 hrs; personal care varied) → **Accept** → scroll **Preview** → downloads → paste → **print**.
- If the client is going into **respite this week**, note it — it's a short change (often 1 week), you'll redo next week.
- **Forgot to print?** No problem — go back to the **dashboard**, the batch is still in the download arrow. It won't show a name (HIPAA), but it's there.

## 3. Missed visits / service interruptions / resolutions (the "Miss Visits" screen)

- **Navigation:** click **Miss Visits**, then pick the date range. For the weekend: **Friday → yesterday**.
- These are ones you **must respond to**. Types:
  - **Service interruption** (e.g., aide out of town for Carolyn)
  - **Declined services** (e.g., someone declined Friday)
  - **Resolutions** (code/exception related — the "2s and 5s")
- **Handling codes/notifications:** when a case manager (or the system) sends a notice that **"2s and 5s codes are seen"**, the main thing is to **ACKNOWLEDGE it and try to resolve it**.
  - Reply: "I'll reach out to the aide and resolve it," note any context (e.g., aide switched to the **new app** this week), **send** → the message clears.
- **Who to call for coverage:** know who each client's aide is; if one of "our people" covers, reach out to them. Some clients are **MAX beneficiaries** — the agency knows who those are.
- Aide list note: e.g., Kay is covered by Deborah Walton (friend of Max) but Deborah may be leaving — stay on top of turnover.

## 5. Employee files — the complete packet

Two folders per employee: one for the **client** side (no tab) and one tabbed (**tasksheets** etc.). Organize **alphabetically**.

### The "gravy" (back) side vs the "Medicaid-checks-constantly" (left) side
- **Medicaid always checks:** health assessment, drug test, driver's license + social, central registry doc, I-9/W-4, sl/offender registries, competency.
- **Left side stuff mostly comes from *them* (state/Medicaid).**

### Documents required (all **date & time-stamped** — must be stamped BEFORE they clock in)
1. **Health assessment** — signed by Chanel (the nurse) and dated.
2. **Drug test** — form filled, signed, dated.
3. **Driver's license + Social Security card.**
4. **Central Registry (central registry of abusers) doc** — print **with headings** so it shows date/time (else sign+date it).
5. **I-9 / W-4** (tax/pay info kept in the very back of the folder = "pay information & tax information" tab).
6. **Sexual offender registry** — trainer pulls this **separately**, date/time stamped, **costs nothing**.
7. **10-year driving record** — date/time stamped. **KEY: check it says NO SUSPENSION.**
   - If **suspended** → still can work BUT must sign a **non-transport agreement** and **cannot work a client that has transportation services**.
8. **SLED / child registry** — date/time stamped, must be done beforehand.
9. **Competency** — see section 6.
10. **In-service** — see section 7.
11. **Resume** — if hired via **Indeed**, print it off Indeed and file it with the paper packet.

### Signatures
- Only **2 pages** the *employee* must sign at hire: the **HIPAA policy** and the **medication policy**. Everything else is already filled/signed.
- **Trainer CAN sign** as trainer, **CANNOT sign as the nurse.**
- **Ink:** must be **black or blue** on employee/legal docs (these are legal documents). Red = only for your own marking (e.g., "Tara down red" on the deficiency list).

### Rehires
- Gone **< ~30 days (short, sweet)** → just redo the **background, health assessment, competencies**.
- Gone **> ~90 days** → redo the **whole packet**.
- **Retention:** keep employee information **5 years from termination date** → label folders with **start + end dates**.

## 6. Competency forms (adults vs children)

- **Adult personal care competency:** **2 signatures**; you (trainer) CAN sign as **instructor + evaluator** and date it.
- **Children's (CPC/A) competency:** **everything must be done by an RN — nothing less.** An LPN can sign **hiring packets**, but children's competencies must be **signed off by an RN**. Practical rule: just use an **RN** for anything with kids.
- Kids' form: can "cheat" it (same topics + same date filled for all) — but it **must be RN-signed**.
- Competency has a **test** in it; keep the form with the test.

## 7. In-service (annual) training docs

- **Annual in-service form:** each **box = 1 hour / 1 credit**. Need **10 credits** (technically), but **7 are automatic** (mandatory yearly topics) + extras → doing the whole page gives more than required (fine).
- **Sign as trainer** → employee signs → **nurse signs off bottom** → put **date, employee name, company name** (always the company name).
- **Topics:** you choose from the required list; base picks on **issues/complaints you saw that year** (e.g., 2025 = **cell phone policy** and **strike policy** because of complaints). They don't care which (within the allowed topics).
- **Timing:** can do all at once (must be before everyone's expires) OR stagger by **hire date**. Trainer does mass-message in **January**: "you have until **Sept 9** to get your in-service done or you don't get a paycheck." (Deadline note: **Sept 9** for current cycle.)
- Forms come from **Phoenix → the "?" (help/question-mark) menu** where all the forms live.
- **Paperwork renewal:** renew each employee's paperwork **yearly on their hire date** — keep a **spreadsheet of every hire date**.

## 8. Payroll coordination (weekly)

- **Call payroll Thursday, before 3 PM** (preferably ~earlier). Read off the **report of everyone's hours**.
- If there's a **new hire**, tell him: "I've got a new person, need to add them."
- **New-hire info he'll ask for:** SSN, DOB, first+last name, address, phone, email, **filing status**, and **direct deposit**.
  - **Direct deposit:** give **account number + routing** — he verifies it, then asks **again** to re-verify. Read them in chunks (trainer does **3 numbers at a time**) to avoid errors. **Mistake = you pay them out of pocket** — slow down and get it right.
- **Tax/filing status:** single / married / head of household. **Deductions** = # of dependents × **$2200**. e.g., **3 children = $6,600** on a W-4. (Trainer personally claims single+0 to maximize withholding.)
- Keep pay + tax info in the **very back** of the folder so it's one grab.

## 9. Filing system (paper)

- **Client folders:** alphabetical by last name. Each client = **2 folders**: (1) actual **client folder** (no tab), (2) **tabbed** folder for **tasksheets**.
  - **Authorizations go on one side, nurse visits on the other.**
  - If a folder gets **too thick**, split/shift into another folder.
- **Aide folders:** alphabetical by last name. **DSN aides** (3 people) live in a **separate little section**.
- **Insert/withdraw:** when pulling a file, put a **green out-card**; there are extras.
- **Task sheets:** drivers/aides with **T&A** get task sheets/cover sheets (the batch you print — the trainer calls them "con sheets"). A **driver's (Diaz's)** task sheets look different from a **PC aide's**.
- Folder sizing: 8.5" pocket folders; there's a groove at 8.5" — you'll feel the click.
- The hole-puncher is **broken on one side** (halfway punches) — punch stacks, not singles.
