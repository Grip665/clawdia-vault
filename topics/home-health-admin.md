## ⚠️ TB Screening Requirement (2026-08-24) — WHERE IT COMES FROM

**Source: S.C. Code Regs. Ch. 61, §61-122-400 (Health Status)** — state law, NOT from our training packet.

> **A.** All staff/caregivers who have contact with clients must have a **health assessment within 12 months prior to initial client contact**, which **shall include tuberculosis screening** per CDC / DPH current TB guidelines.
> **B.** In-home care providers conduct an **annual TB risk assessment** (Appendix) to set screening frequency.

**What it means for us (plain English):**
- TB screening is **built into the new health assessment** (the one Chanel/RN signs) — not a separate standalone test.
- **New hire:** baseline TB screen **before any client contact**. Baseline = **2-step PPD (TST) or single blood assay (IGRA/QuantiFERON)**. A documented negative in the last 12 months → single test suffices.
- **After hire:** **NO annual TB test in low-risk setting** — just the **annual risk assessment**.
- **Positive test / symptoms** → chest X-ray + medical evaluation.
- **Where to get:** primary care, urgent care, occupational health, public health clinic, or CVS/Walgreens clinic. **QuantiFERON (IGRA blood draw)** = fastest for new hires (no 48-hr return like PPD).
- **Status (08-24):** 🔴 **2 new hires need baseline TB screen before client contact** — need to figure out how/where to run it (NOT yet scheduled).

## 2. Authorizations & terminations (Home Care)

- You can print **terminations first** or the **big ones** first — order is preference. (Trainer is "a terminations person.")
- **Why 4 items for 1 client:** a client with **2 services** (companion + personal care) = **2 cancels + 2 restarts** = 4.
- **Workflow:** get the auth number (save off email or the list) → **Terminations → top one → Preview** → it downloads (top-right arrow) → **Cmd/Ctrl+Z paste** → Enter → it pulls them up.
- **Always print on ANY change** — even same authorizations/dates. If an **address or phone** changed → **HIGHLIGHT** the change and file it. Highlight any change so it's obvious later.
- **Accepting an authorization:** check schedule → (companion typically 2 hrs; personal care varied) → **Accept** → scroll **Preview** → downloads → paste → **print**.
- If the client is going into **respite this week**, note it — it's a short change (often 1 week), you'll redo next week.
- **Forgot to print?** No problem — go back to the **dashboard**, the batch is still in the download arrow. It won't show a name (HIPAA), but it's there.

### Fingerprint / background check — SC (hiring administrator's job)
- **Residency-based, NOT universal** (corrected 08-11, per statute § 44-7-2910):
  - Aide who **verifies 12 mo SC residency** (SC license/ID, rent/mortgage/utility, pay stubs, SC bank records) → **SLED state check only** is lawful. No fingerprints needed.
  - Aide who **can't verify SC residency** → state check + **federal fingerprint (FBI)** check.
  - Verified 12-mo residency in another state → may run only that state's check.
- So fingerprints are required for aides who **can't document SC residency** — universal prints is agency policy, not the statute. (Medicaid waivers/payer contracts can impose more.)
- **When the federal/print check IS needed (your steps):**
  1. Ensure a **valid relationship** with the applicant (SLED directive).
  2. Complete the **"Verification of Application and Authorization for Background Check"** form → email **HQCBC@dph.sc.gov**.
  3. Give the applicant the **Noncriminal Justice Applicant's Privacy Rights** + **FBI Privacy Act Statement** (mandatory).
  4. DPH returns a **service code** → send the aide to **IdentoGO** for live-scan prints.
  5. File the **result / DPH eligibility determination** (as received + proof of check initiation).
- **Fee:** ~**$51.75** IdentoGO; either party may pay (SC AG). Absorbing it is the recruiting-friendly choice.
- **Agency-level (separate, NOT aides):** 42 CFR § 455.434/.450 — fingerprint check on the **provider + 5%+ owners** (home health = high-risk, revalidate every **3 yrs**); FCBC enrollment requirement since **Aug 15, 2022**. At revalidation, submit fingerprint appointment confirmation to avoid 30-day termination.
- **Legacy aides — sort files:** (a) state check + residency documented = likely compliant; (b) state check but residency never verified = federal check should have been run; (c) no check = violation. Fix (b)/(c): run checks NOW + **dated corrective-action memo** (never quietly backfill). Break ≥1 yr = re-run check before resuming. Missing checks = Reg 61-77 survey deficiency; Medicaid overpayment exposure if aides serve Medicaid clients. Attorney before next survey/revalidation.

## 🕒 OFFICE HOURS — DPH vs SCDHHS (corrected 2026-10-30)
**STANDING DECISION (Larry, 2026-10-30): keep M–F 10:00–4:00. Change nothing.** This section is the
ammunition if anyone ever asks why those hours exist or whether DPH requires them.

### SHORT ANSWER
- **DPH licensing sets NO hours.** R.60-122 contains zero time-of-day references.
- **SCDHHS DOES set hours** — personal care providers must be open and staffed 10:00–4:00 M–F, phone
  8:30–5:00 M–F, plus an after-hours emergency number. **Failure = sanctions.**
- So the hours come from **SCDHHS**, not DPH. **Never take down the posted hours on the theory that
  "the regs don't require them"** — that would create a sanctionable violation.

### 1. DPH — R.60-122, *Standards for Licensing In-Home Care Providers*
- Statute **S.C. Code §44-70-10 et seq.**, Document **5407**.
- **Citation detail:** the header box carries BOTH `Proposed in State Register Volume and Issue: 49/9`
  and `Final in State Register Volume and Issue: 50/5`. Cite **50/5** (final publication). 49/9 is the
  proposal — don't cite it.
- 🚫 **Do NOT cite** `110 Day Review Expiration Date for Automatic Approval: 05/03/2026`. That is the
  Administrative Procedures Act automatic-approval trigger, **not** an expiry. Quoting it invites the
  misreading "the regulation expired in May."
- **No clock hours anywhere** — grep for `[0-9]:[0-9]{2}`, `A.M.`, `P.M.` returns **0 hits**.
- What it *does* require: primary office **in-state**, commercially zoned (or unzoned + county/city
  letter) **§103.D.1**; definition of primary office **§102.S**; license posted in a public area
  **§103.C.1**; records readily accessible **§601.A.2**.
- Closest thing to an hours rule — **§202.A**: *"All providers are subject to inspection and/or
  investigation without prior notice. When staff members are not present, the provider shall provide
  information as to the expected return of staff."*

### 2. R.61-122 — the PREDECESSOR (superseded)
- **Same title**, same statute. DHEC-era, June 27 2014, six pages.
- ⚠️ **CORRECTION (2026-10-30):** an earlier note in this vault described R.61-122 as a *different
  provider category* (home health agencies / skilled). **That was wrong.** It is the old version of the
  same regulation. **Chapter 61 = DHEC numbering; Chapter 60 = DPH numbering.**
- **§103.J** is where the "9 to 5" language lives: *"…accessible in person, by phone, or page during
  the hours of 9:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M., Monday through Friday, except for those holidays recognized by
  the State of South Carolina."*
- **NOT carried into R.60-122.** Anyone quoting 9–5 as a *current* DPH requirement is quoting the
  superseded 2014 text.

### 3. SCDHHS — where the hours actually come from
Personal Care Services Scope, **eff. 7/1/2025**, provider administrative standards, **item 8**:
> "The provider must ensure that its office is **open and staffed by qualified personnel during the
> hours of 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.** Outside of these hours, the Provider agency
> must be **available by telephone during normal business hours, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through
> Friday.** Failure to maintain an open and staffed office as indicated **will result in sanctions** as
> outlined in section G, last paragraph. The provider must also have a **number for emergencies outside
> of normal business hours.**"

- This is **mandatory**, not a self-declaration.
- Our practice matches it exactly: 10:00–4:00 open+staffed · 8:30–5:00 by phone · 24/7 emergency
  (864) 652-5100. Admin doc `03_Policy_Business_Hours_and_Office_Coverage`.
- Related but separate: HCBS Provider Manual §8 reserves the right to do onsite reviews *"during
  normal business hours"* — that's about inspections, not a duty to be open.
- Review #44601 (admin) recorded our declared hours as M–F 10AM–4PM, open and staffed = Yes.

### If asked "does DPH require us to keep office hours?"
Answer: **no — DPH licensing doesn't. SCDHHS does, and that's the requirement we comply with.**

- Requires a **primary office** only in the structural sense: "The main office … where all records are kept, secured, and accessible, and from which a provider performs oversight, administrative, and coordination of services" (Definitions §102.S).
