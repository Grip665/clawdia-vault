## What Is Vibe Coding?

Using AI tools (Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Codex) to build software by describing what you want in natural language. You act as a "coach" to AI agents rather than writing code line by line. Term coined by Andrej Karpathy (Feb 2025).

## Key Stat

**45% of vibe coders don't have CS degrees.** Companies care about what you can ship, not your major. Larry's BS in Sports Management is fine.

## What Companies Look For

1. Can you use AI tools (Claude, Cursor, Copilot) to build working software?
2. Can you take an ambiguous idea and turn it into something real fast?
3. Do you understand workflows and *why* processes need to work a certain way?
4. Strong written communication (most teams are async/remote)
5. Self-direction and ownership
