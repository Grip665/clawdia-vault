01.10       Introduction


            The South Carolina Department of Health and Human Services (SCDHHS)
            Centralized Intake process ensures that all persons with perceived long-term care needs
            receive every opportunity for access to Community Long Term Care (CLTC) waivers
            and programs. The process identifies persons who meet intake criteria and serves as an
            information and referral source for those who do not meet intake criteria.


            Individuals may have more than one CLTC program application open at a time if the
            situation requires and the appropriate criteria are met. It is important for SCDHHS
            workers to verify any other program applications and overall status in Phoenix prior to
            initiating and ending case actions.

01.20       Intake Policy and Procedures

    01.21      Referral Sources

                   A referral to SCDHHS may originate from any knowledgeable source such as:

                   1.     Applicant
                   2.     Family member
                   3.     Interested neighbor or friend
                   4.     Physician
                   5.     Hospital staff
                   6.     Nursing home staff
                   7.     Staff of a public or private agency
                   8.     Medicare – Medicaid Plan (PRIME)


                   Agencies that might make referrals include, but are not limited to, the Department
                   of Social Services, Department of Health and Human Services, Department of
                   Health and Environmental Control, Aging Agencies, Department of Mental Health,
                   Department of Disabilities and Special Needs, and private home health agencies.

    01.22      Confidentiality

                   Information about Medicaid applicants and recipients shall be safeguarded in
                   accordance with the provisions of the Department of Health and Human Services
                   regulations governing confidentiality and HIPAA (Regulations 126-170, et, seq.,).
                   Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:

                   1.     The name and address of the individual served and the service received;
                   2.     Medical data, including diagnosis and history of disease or disability;
                   3.     Agency evaluation of information about a person;
                   4.     The identity of persons or institutions that furnished health services to a person;
                   5.     The social and economic conditions or circumstances of any person served;
                   6.     Federal program information identified as confidential by the appropriate
                          federal authority

    01.23          Coverage of Intake Duties

                   Intake duties are performed by Centralized Intake. Arrangements should be made
                   to ensure that intake is covered at all times from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on business
                   days and that an intake team member can be reached to handle any urgent intake
                   issues.

                   Centralized Intake will provide administrative assistance in obtaining application
                   information. This includes the following activities:

                   •   Recording reported functional deficit(s)

                   •   Checking the system for existing records to prevent duplication

                   •   Keying, scanning, and documenting all appropriate information

                   •   Checking the Medicaid system for Medicaid Eligibility and creating new eligibility

                   •   Processing Medicaid Eligibility approvals/denials for waiver applicants

                   Once the applicant has been determined to meet the intake criteria and Medicaid
                   Eligibility has been verified, the case will be released to the Area Office.


    01.24      Timeliness Standard for Intake of a Referral

                   Referrals received through Centralized Intake must be processed within 3 business
                   days following receipt of the referral.

    01.25      Referrals

                   Centralized Intake receives referrals by telephone and electronically.

                   Telephone Referral

                   The applicant, family member or interested individual can make a referral via a
                   toll-free telephone number (888-971-1637) to initiate an application. The phone
                   line hours are as follows:
                                Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
                                     Wednesday and Friday 8:30 a.m. - 12:30 p.m.

                   Electronic Referral

                   The applicant, family member, interested individual or professional may make an
                   electronic referral by entering the necessary information at the website:
                   https://phoenix.scdhhs.gov/eref/

                   Note:       If a walk-in referral is received, the SCDHHS Area Office will provide
                               the referral source with the Centralized Intake contact information to
                               make either a telephone or electronic referral and assist as needed.

                   When the referral is received, Centralized Intake:

                   •   checks for an existing record in Phoenix.

                   Note:       If a case is currently active in Phoenix, determines if the referral is an
                               application for a different program;

                   •   completes related contacts to the referral source, applicant, and/or Authorized
                       Representative, as needed to accurately obtain ADL information.

                   •   follows document scanning policies. Refer to Documentation policy and scan tag
                       description;

                   •   consults with a SCDHHS Central Office Nurse to confirm functional dependencies
                       needed;

                   •   enters application data in Phoenix as appropriate.

                   If Centralized Intake requires additional information to process a referral and is
                   unable to reach the applicant/ Authorized Representative/referral source, a CLTC
                   Notification Form giving a ten (10) business day notice to call Centralized Intake
                   Staff must be mailed. This notice will not include the Appeals Notice. If no
                   response is received within ten (10) business days, the program application should
                   be terminated as “Intake Criteria Not Met”. All actions taken will be documented
                   in the narrative.

    01.26      Intake Criteria

                   The following criteria must be met for a referral to be considered appropriate for
                   intake:

                   1. Applicant who is requesting waiver services from the Community Choices
                      Program must be 18 years old or older (stated age is acceptable) or must turn 18

                      Medicaid-sponsored nursing home placement has no age restriction.

                   2. Applicant must be a South Carolina resident or must be currently located in South

                           b.      Toileting

                           a. Locomotion
                           b. Transfer
                           c. Bowel or bladder continence

                   Note:        An applicant who is requesting Medicaid sponsored nursing home
                                placement does not require a functional dependency for intake
                                processing.

     01.27         Applicants Who Do Not Meet Intake Criteria

                   When a person does not meet intake criteria, Centralized Intake will notify the
                   applicant of this decision in writing via the CLTC Notification Form including the
                   Appeals Notice.


                   Centralized Intake will consult with a SCDHHS Central Office Nurse if it appears
                   the applicant does not meet intake criteria and the applicant wants to continue with
                   the application.

                   The SCDHHS Central Office Nurse will determine if the applicant meets the intake
                   criteria.

                   If the applicant does not meet intake criteria, the SCDHHS Central Office Nurse will:

    01.29      Application Release to the SCDHHS Area Office

                   Once an applicant has been determined to meet intake criteria and has been
                   determined financially eligible for the program, Centralized Intake will release
                   the application to the SCDHHS Area Office for assignment. An SCDHHS worker
                   will document who the case was assigned to in the Area Office.


     01.30    Duplicates/Rejection

                   Centralized Intake will reject additional referrals made on an applicant if there is
                   an active application for the applicant, for the same waiver/program, when the
                   referral is processed. Referrals will also be rejected when Centralized Intake is
                   notified by the referral source that a referral was submitted in error (for example,
                   the referrer states the referral was made for the wrong person or the wrong
                   program).


                   Multiple Nursing Home referrals/applications are acceptable in certain situations
                   such as one being for conversion and another coming in for placement or multiple
                   conversion applications with different conversion (Level of Care) dates.

    01.31      Case Transfer Policy

                   All Waiver applications are auto assigned by Phoenix. All Nursing Home
                   Placement applications will be transferred by Centralized Intake to the SCDHHS
                   Area Office where the applicant resides (per the current address listed on the
                   referral). If it is a PACE Only application, then the application goes to the PACE
                   program area.


                   If it is a standard desk review assessment for Nursing Home Placement, the
                   SCDHHS Area Office (per residential address) will process the application. If the
                   applicant will need to be seen face to face, the application may be transferred to
                   the SCDHHS Area Office where the applicant is currently located for that
                   SCDHHS Area Office to process the application.


                   If an applicant has applications for multiple programs, all applications will go the
                   SCDHHS Area Office where the applicant resides and that SCDHHS Area Office
                   can transfer the application to other areas if needed.


                   Centralized Intake will only transfer cases during intake. Once the case is in the
                   Area Office, the Area Office has the ability to transfer to other area offices as
                   needed, with proper notification to the other Area Office that the case is being
                   transferred.
