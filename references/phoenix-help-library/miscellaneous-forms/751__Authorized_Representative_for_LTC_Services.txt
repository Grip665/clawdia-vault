                                               Appointing an Authorized Representative

Would you like to allow someone to receive and provide information on your behalf for the long term care services?
You can give a trusted person permission to talk with Community Long Term Care (CLTC) or Long Term Care workers about you and
see your information. This person will act on your behalf to provide and receive information regarding your medical condition, how
you perform daily activities such as bathing, your service needs, and the company you would like to provide services. This person
may also act on your behalf for appeals. This person is called an “Authorized Representative.” CLTC or Long Term Care workers can
release any information regarding your application process to the people you indicate. Up to three (3) people can serve as your
Authorized Representative. You can appoint, withdraw, or change an Authorized Representative at any time. If you ever need to
change your Authorized Representative, you can contact the local CLTC office.
Please check the appropriate box and enter information for the AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE.
