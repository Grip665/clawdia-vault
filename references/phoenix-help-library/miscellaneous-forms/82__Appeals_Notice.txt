                           APPEALS NOTICE
As a Medicaid applicant/recipient, you have the right to a fair hearing
regarding this decision. To initiate the appeal process, you or your
representative must submit a written request stating the issue you wish to
appeal along with a copy of the attached Notification Form. The request
must be sent to the following address no later than thirty (30) days from
the receipt of this notification.
Division of Appeals and Fair Hearings
Department of Health and Human Services
Post Office Box 8206
Columbia, South Carolina 29202-8206
Provider/Beneficiary may file an appeal electronically at
www.scdhhs.gov/appeals.

You are eligible to receive continued benefits/services pending a hearing
decision. If you are interested in continued benefits you must request a
hearing before the indicated date of action. Please notify your CLTC
representative that you want your services continued. If the hearing
officer’s decision is not in your favor, federal regulations allow South
Carolina Department of Health and Human Services to seek repayment
of the cost of Medicaid benefits received while waiting on the decision.
Unless a request is made within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt of
this notification, this decision will be final and binding. A request for a
fair hearing is considered filed if postmarked by the thirtieth (30th)
calendar day following receipt of this notification.
