Know that CLTC is the approved abbreviation for Community Long Term Care.

Scope of Services
      Know the minimum in-service training hours (10) required for all Case Managers that do not have professional
       licenses. Located in Case Management Scope of Services.

      Know that Case Managers are not employees of the Department of Health and Human Services or Community
       Long Term Care.

      Know how often Case Managers are required to check emails Monday-Friday and the time frame for returning
       phone calls.

      Know what hours during the day case manager can make contacts and visits to participant.


Phoenix
      Know that Phoenix is the name for Case Management Software System.

      Know how to Access Phoenix.

      Know where to locate in Phoenix on-going resources and practice training opportunities for Case Managers.

      Know what Phoenix Tabs CM is to review or update during a home visit.

      Know that Case Manager is required to sign the Re-evaluation assessment in Phoenix.


Eligibility for Waivered Services
      Know that all CLTC Waiver Enrollees must meet Level of Care and have SC Medicaid.

      Know the minimum age requirement to enroll in the Community Choice Waiver. (Age 18)


Level of Care
      Know who completes the initial assessment/Level of Care.

      Know the meaning of functional Deficit and how it relates to Level of Care.

      Know the two types of Level of Care. (Skilled and Intermediate)

      Know that a participant who is total care is considered skilled.

Case Management
   Know how often Case Manager must monitor each participant in his/her caseload.

   Know the process for entering the Emergency Preparedness Disaster plan for participant.

   Know the Emergency Disaster Priority Levels for participant.

   Know when an initial visit is due after waiver enrollment.

   Know the activities CM completes during an Initial Visit.

   Know what forms should be signed in Phoenix during the initial visit.

   Know when a Re-evaluation is due for participant enrolled in Waiver. (365 day intervals)

   Know the policy regarding a participant leaving the state temporarily and receiving services.

   Know the difference between Primary Contact and Caregiver. Know where to record contact/caregiver
    information Phoenix.

   Know which individual may not be a provider of CLTC Waivered services.

   Know what APS stands for. (Adult Protective Services)

   Know how often CM should monitor an Adult Protective Services complaint that is opened in Phoenix.

   Know how often CM should monitor progress and collaborate with APS.

   Know how often CM must address Personal Goals with participant.

   Know where to find a blank Estate Recovery Form.

   Know when a signed Estate Recovery Notification Form is required.

Waivered Services
    Know which waivered service is available to all participants. (Community Case Management)


    Know what waiver services are available to participant in Community Choice Waiver, HIV/AIDS Waiver and
     Ventilator Waiver.

    Know what waivered services participant can receive while enrolled in Hospice services.

    Know where to generate a Participant Service Choice Form in Phoenix.

    Know which waiver services require the Case Manager to obtain the medical doctor order before services can
     be authorized.

    Know which waiver services Providers are required to obtain the medical doctor order before services can be
     authorized.

    Know that the Waiver Support Tab in Phoenix is where you will find Open Authorization information for
     participant.

    Know that the Non-Waiver Tab in Phoenix is where you will find services participant is receiving that is not a
     waiver service. Example Home Health.

    Know how many days CM have to issue an authorization once a referral is accepted by provider and a start date
     determined.

    Know that Environmental Modification Services have a lifetime cap of $7,500.

    Be able to give an example of a participant who is at risk for a missed Personal Care Aide Visit.

    Know that participant/primary contact must make a choice of provider for each waivered services and CM must
     not influence participant/primary contact with choice.

    Know that not all waivered services have a scope of services.

    Know where in Phoenix CM should add/change participant’s Caregiver Support.

Service Plan
         Know where the service plan agreement form is located in Phoenix.

         Known how often the service plan agreement form should be signed in Phoenix.

         Know how often a copy of the service plan should be given to participant based on policy and procedure.

         Know that the service plan includes goals, interventions and needs.

Documentation
         Know how many days a Case Manager has to enter Case Management Activity documentation into Phoenix.

         Know the policy regarding texting and emailing methods for communication between CLTC
          representative/workers and participants, primary contact, providers and other related agencies.

         Know when to use the conversation in Phoenix.

         Know that CM is required to complete a narrative checklist for each CM activity. (Initial Visit, Monthly
          Contact, Quarterly Visits, Re-evaluation)

         Be able to write a narrative sample for an appropriately documented monthly contact.

Termination of Participant from CLTC Waiver
        Know reason for terminating participant from CLTC Waiver.

        Know what form to send when terminating a participant from the CLTC Waiver.

        Know who should receive a copy of termination notification.

        Know the termination date for participant who dies while enrolled in CLTC Waiver. (Date of Death)

        Know the process for terminating a participant who becomes financially ineligible.

        Know the appeals process and participant right to request an appeal.
