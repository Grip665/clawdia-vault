 Training Environment

 • Automatic setup: Device Id’s copied from Production every 20 minutes
       during the business day. (No device id or worker set up needed.)
 • Training scenarios shared statewide for all users.
 • 30 minute checkout window. Scenario self clears every 30 minutes.
 • Multiple sets of same scenarios for common services (e.g. In-Home Agency)
 • Phone location is simulated – does not use actual GPS coordinates
 • All scenario worker id passwords are Pass22!!
 • User can lock themselves out or change passwords. Both are reset every
       20 minutes during the business day.
 • Some presentation differences between IVR, Android, and iOS

Q/R Card                                          Sample Letter and Card:

Use: Clients with no land line or cell coverage
     Worker needs to check in and out with
      no data service available
     Card stays in Client home
     Worker scans card at check in and out

Process:
• Option to order card is presented to the
    worker when checking in or out
• Worker orders card from Mobile App
• Worker explains card to Client or family
    member
• Card arrives in US Mail 5-10 days later
• Client uses magnet to attach to
    refrigerator or somewhere handy
• Worker scans card at check in and
    check out

 in and out today, you should have data
 coverage and not need a Q/R card.

   Tips and Tricks


Key Tips, Tricks, and Notes
  • Get both your phones and workers registered in Phoenix now

  • Use the menu button on the phone to get the Device Id in the app
     (NOT the “about this phone” page in settings)
  • Device Id’s have 0’s and 1’s, NOT O’s and I’s. Apple Id’s have hyphens.

  • On Android, when the user changes password on the phone during the
     work day, the OLD password works for the rest of the day. The new
     password takes over the next day. (This will be changed in new release.)
  • On iOS, user password changes take effect immediately.

  • Always log into the app in an area where you have good data signal at the
      beginning of the day and the end of the day.
  • If you don’t have data connection when you want to check in or out, try
       to move somewhere where you do connect. Step outside if necessary.

  • Practice, practice, practice on the Training site. There are scenarios for
      all the main types of services.

  • Report problems so we can fix them and make this successful.
