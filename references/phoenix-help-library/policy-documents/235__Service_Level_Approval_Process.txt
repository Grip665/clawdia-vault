                SERVICE LEVEL APPROVAL PROCESS
 for Area Administrators, Lead Team Members and State Reviewers

If the Participant is not authorized for Adult Day Health Care OR the Participant
is authorized for Adult Day Health Care two (2) days or less per week, the
following service combinations require Central Office approval:

   • State reviewer any combination up to 20 hours
   • AA/LT any combination up to 40 hours
   • CO any combination greater than 40 hours

                            Combination of Services
                                Personal Care 1
                                Personal Care 2
                               Agency Companion
                             Individual Companion
                                Attendant Care


If the Participant is authorized for Adult Day Health Care three (3) days or more
per week, the following service combinations require Central Office approval:

   • State reviewer any combination up to 15 hours
   • AA/LT any combination up to 35 hours
   • CO any combination greater than 35 hours.

                            Combination of Services
                                Personal Care 1
                                Personal Care 2
                               Agency Companion
                             Individual Companion
                                Attendant Care
