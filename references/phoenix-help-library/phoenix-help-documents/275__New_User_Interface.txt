 UI Sidebar Navigation
                          Sidebar Navigation
                           Displays the (Dashboard, Participants, Providers,
                          Users, Workers, and Claims) sections on the side of
                          the screen.

                                                   Sidebar Navigation
                                                   Each section expands
                                                   into subsections by
                                                   clicking the arrow.
                                                   When a new section is
                                                   expanded, any other
                                                   section that has been
                                                   expanded will collapse
                                                   or condense.

                     Background Color
                     Click the Paint Palette icon to change the
                     background and avatar color scheme. The
                     Paint Palette icon is located next to the
                     Phoenix logo at the bottom of the screen
                     (except the dashboard). Hover the mouse
                     over the area next to the Phoenix login for
                     the Paint Palette icon to be appear
