            Assigning Caregiver Tasks
   Select “Frequency” and “Start” date then click “Save”

   To create additional tasks, select another task and click “Add task”

            Assigning Caregiver Tasks
  If caregiver(s) is performing “Hands on care”, click “Primary” for the caregiver
   that will be considered the primary caregiver. Note: There can be more than 1
   primary caregiver
  Click “Save”




4/28/2020
               Caregiver Interview
  When a caregiver is performing “Hands on care” task and is the “Primary”
   caregiver, an interview questionnaire MUST be answered. Note: The
   Interview questionnaire measures the primary caregiver’s stress level
  Under the caregiver’s tab, click “Interviews” tab




4/28/2020
              Caregiver Interview
  Under the “Interview” tab, complete the interview questions
  Select interview date and time then click “Save Interview”




4/28/2020
                Caregiver Interview
  Once the interview questionnaire is complete, a total stress level will be
    displayed

                    Caregiver Supports
                        Permission
  Caregiver’s Permission gives CLTC/Contract agents permission to speak to a designated
   person(s) regarding the participant
  Under the caregiver’s tab, click “New Permission” sub-tab
  Check the person(s) that have permission or uncheck the person(s) that do not have
   permission then click “Continue”
  Caregivers that have permission can be viewed by clicking “Current/Previous
   Permissions”
