                 Logging into Pheonix
 Enter username into the “Username” field Note: Username can contain a maximum
  of 8 character
 Enter password into the “Password” field Note: Passwords are case sensitive.
  Passwords must be 8 to 40 characters long and contain 3 of 4 entities (upper case,
  lower case, number, and symbol)
     e.g. (Karlpop2, sandwich$7, pirate@50, Bob%Smith)
     Changing/resetting passwords (see “Editing User Information”)
 Solve math problem then click “Continue”. After 3 unsuccessful attempts, the user will
  be locked out and will need to contact the Phoenix helpdesk for assistance or wait 30
  minutes to login.

 “Help” screens are available in certain sections of Phoenix. The “Help” screen

                        Comments
 Comment boxes are displayed at the bottom of certain sections
  Comments boxes are used for documenting information that is not a
  choice, can not be explained in the section, or additional information.
  i.e. (medications that is not listed in Phoenix, notes documented
  in the assessment after it has been completed, accepting an
  authorization with conditions)
 Enter comments in the “Comment” box then click “Save/Add
  Comment” Note: Comments are automatically signed and dated

                  Report Problem
 Technical issue(s) such as (problems with authorizations, syncing,
  dashboards, service plans, etc.) can be reported to the Phoenix Help
  Desk by clicking the “Report a Problem”
    Do not report technical issues in the Phoenix “Complaint” section
 The “Report a Problem” button is located at the bottom of each
  screen




                                                                         21
                     Report Problem
 At the “New Issue” screen, fill out the appropriate information in each field
  then click “Submit your issue”. Note: DO NOT include any PHI (Medicaid
  number, SSN, Name, or Date of Birth) in the description box
 Users will receive an e-mail documenting problem reported and any questions
  that may require a response to resolve issue(s)
 Don’t send a reply e-mail or click on the link in e-mail
 Any additional responses, go to “View My Issues” from the Phoenix Home
  Screen
