                                      Referrals

▪Reminder: Electronic referrals can only be made for services included in the service plan
▪A “proposed” service schedule is entered for the referral information
▪If a provider declines a referral or does not respond within 48 hours, Phoenix automatically
sends the referral to the next provider.
▪Phoenix checks the status of referrals at the top of every hour
▪Providers can accept, decline, or ignore a referral
▪When the provider accepts a referral, the CM needs to “Accept” the provider. If the provider
accepts, comments can be included which might change the start date.
▪Authorization should be created from referrals that they are associated and NOT by selecting a
“New Authorization”




                                                                                        2
                        Service Authorization

▪If a service is not included in the service plan it CANNOT be authorized.
▪If you need to authorize a service that is not included in the service plan, you need to
edit the service plan to include interventions for service.
▪For electronic referrals, the schedule on the authorization defaults to what was in the
referral and can be changed
▪When a referral for Environmental Modification, Ramps, or Enhanced Pest Control is
accepted, the case manager will not create the service authorization until the work
has been completed
▪Once the work has been completed, the case manger will complete the
authorization

                                   Referrals
• Notification will be sent to the 1st Proposed Provider’s dashboard awaiting action
• Proposed Provider can accept, decline, or accept the referral with conditions such
  as (starting on a different date)
• 1st Proposed Provider will have 2 business days excluding weekends and State
  holidays to accept referral or the referral will be forfeited and sent to the next
  Proposed Provider
• If the referral is sent to the next Proposed Provider, then the referral acceptance
  process start over again until all Proposed Providers options are exhausted
      • Participants should be encouraged to select multiple Providers from the
        Provider Choice List to ensure finding a Provider to perform the service(s)
