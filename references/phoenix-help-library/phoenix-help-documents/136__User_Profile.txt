                            Login Screen
 Enter username into the “Username” field Note: Username can contain a maximum
  of 8 character
 Enter password into the “Password” field Note: Passwords are case sensitive.
  Passwords must be 8 to 40 characters long and contain 3 of 4 entities (upper case,
  lower case, number, and symbol)
     e.g. (Karlpop2, sandwich$7, pirate@50, Bob%Smith)
     Changing passwords (see “Changing User Password”)
 Solve math problem then click “Continue”. After 3 unsuccessful attempts, the user will
  be locked out and will need to contact the Phoenix helpdesk for assistance or wait 30
  minutes to login.
