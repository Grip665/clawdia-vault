 From the Phoenix “Add Claims” screen. Users can enter the default information. Click
  “Apply Defaults”
 Any default information entered will auto populate in the claim entry section below.
 Enter all or the remaining information that was not auto populated

 Units will calculate automatically. Select “Save” under the “Action” dropdown box
 If finished, click “Submit”.
