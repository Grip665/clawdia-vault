                     Assessment/Service Plan Changes
Service Plan
Personal Goals Tab
• The Personal Goals tab has been relocated to the
  service plan.
• Users do not have to complete the “Personal Goals”
  tab in the order that the tabs are displayed. Users
  must complete the “Personal Goals” tab before the
  Service Plan can be signed off
• Personal Goals must be addressed in order to
  complete the service plan
• Users must answer the question “ Were personal goals
  discussed with the participant and/or designee during the
  assessment and service planning process?”

                       Assessment/Service Plan Changes
Service Plan
Personal Goals Tab
• If the user was unable to discuss the personal goals,
  then the user will click “No”
• A comment box will display. The user will need to
  enter a comment to explain why the personal goals
  were not discussed
    •   The explanation must me at least 25 characters in
        order to save
    •   The “Save” button will not be available to click until
        explanation reaches 25 characters
• Click “Save”

Service Plan
Personal Goals Tab
• Personal Goals should be SMART
    •   S – Specific
    •   M – Measurable
    •   A – Achievable
    •   R – Relevant
    •   T – Time-limited

  “Steps”

        and Complete” a personal goal
