                Creating an Assessment
  From the “Assessments” screen, select program from drop box. Note: Only the programs
     with an open “Application” will be displayed as a choice
  Enter date in the “Assessed on” Field
  Select the appropriate “Assessment Type” (Face to Face, Phone, or Paper Review)
  Click “Create Assessment” Note: Assessments are tailored to the program (i.e. HIV
     has all sections, NHT has less sections)

            Creating an Assessment
  The “Assessment” screen contain tabs and sub tabs
  On the Assessment screen, ALL the red lights for each tab
     must be green in order to have a completed assessment




4/23/2020
            Creating an Assessment
  At the bottom of each Assessment tab, there are two options:
       “Save”- Save function allow the user to save the data entered, make edits,
        visit other sections, and complete the assessment later. Note: The lights
        will remain red until the section is “Save and Complete”
       “Save and Complete” - Saves data on each completed Assessment tab.
        The lights will turn green when the section is “Save and Complete” Note:
        Edits can be made to Assessment tabs until the Assessment is signed
        and saved

            Assessment-Skin/Nutrition
  Under the “Skin Condition” sub tab, a front and back diagram of the human body
   is available to mark any skin condition
      Click on skin condition below then mark on the body where the occurrence is
        present. Note: Whatever Skin condition is marked on the body, it MUST
        be selected on the Skin Condition menu below
  Previous information will be displayed if any
  Comments can be made in the “Comments” section
  When finish, click “Save” or “Save and Complete”

4/23/2020
                 Assessment-Signature
  The assessor fills out the “Assessor Signature and Date” field then the
   reviewer fills out the “Review Signature and Date” field. Note: The assessor
   can not complete the assessment until the reviewer signs in the “Review
   Signature and Date” field.
  Once the Assessor and Review has signed and dated, click “Save” or “Save and
   Complete” “Save and Complete” function will turn the light green
  Not using the recommended LOC may require a 3rd signature from Central
   office




4/23/2020
             Assessment-Signature
  The Assessor and Reviewer do not have to sign the
   Assessment from the same computer.
  Once the Assessor signs the “Assessor Signature and
   Date” field then the Review can open the assessment and
   sign the “Review Signature and Date” field from another
   computer
  Once the “Review Signature and Date” is signed, click
   “Save and Complete”
