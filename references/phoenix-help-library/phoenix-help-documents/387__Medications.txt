• On the carousel, click “Medications”
• Click “Add medication”




                                         2
                      Adding Medications
• Enter the Medication in the “Search” field. Note: Keying the letters of the
  medication will generate a search result of medications beginning with the same
  letters
• Select Medication
• Select frequency from drop down box
• Add any comments in the comment section then click “Add medication”




                                                                               3
                      Adding Medications
• Selected medication(s) and information will be displayed
• Additional medication (s) can be added by clicking the “Add medication” button
• Medication(s) can be removed by clicking the “Remove Medication”
• Additional comments can be made in the comment section




                                                                                   4
                      Flagged Medications
• Medications that requires monitoring can be flagged by clicking the flag icon
• Flagged medication(s) will be highlighted
• Flagged medication(s) can be undone by clicking the “Flag” button
• Additional comments can be made in the comment section




                                                                                  5
                Discontinued Medications
• Medication can be discontinued clicking the “DC” button
• Enter date in the “Discontinued on” section
• Click the “Discontinue” button
• Additional comments can be made in the comment section
