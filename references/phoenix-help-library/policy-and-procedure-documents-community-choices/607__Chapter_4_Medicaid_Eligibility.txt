04.10           Introduction

              The eligibility process for CLTC waiver applicants is completed through
              coordination between the CLTC office and the Medicaid Eligibility office. Waiver
              applicants must meet the financial and categorical criteria for Medicaid and meet
              level of care for home and community-based services initially and on an on-going
              basis as a participant.

04.20      Medicaid Financial Eligibility Determination Process

      Verifying an applicant's Medicaid financial and categorical eligibility is an important
      function of the CLTC Centralized Intake Program Assistant in the pre-admission process.

      1.       If an applicant is not presently eligible for any Medicaid Category and is seeking
               Medicaid-sponsorship in a long term care facility or community- based services, an
               application for Medicaid financial and categorical eligibility must be made with the
               Medicaid Eligibility Office.

               The Centralized Intake Program Assistant mails the Medicaid Eligibility
               Application for Nursing Home, Residential or In-Home Care Form 3401 to the
               applicant. The waiver application will not be processed until financial eligibility is
               determined.

      2.       If an applicant may be eligible for Medicaid for any category other than Category 80,
               86, 16, 54, 17, 18, 10, 15, 19 (SSI) or 33 ABD/NH and is seeking community-based
               services, an application for Medicaid financial and categorical eligibility must be
               made with the Medicaid Eligibility Office. The waiver application will not be
               processed until financial eligibility is determined.

               Centralized Intake mails the Medicaid Eligibility Application for Nursing Home,
               Residential or In-Home Care Form 3401 or Form 3400 B to the applicant.

               The applicant will return the completed Form to SCDHHS Central Mail Center, PO
               Box 100101, Columbia, SC 29202-3101.

      3.       If an applicant is presently eligible for Medicaid Category 80, 86, 15, 16, 54, 17, 18,
               19, and 10 (SSI) or 33 ABD/NH and is seeking community based services,
               Centralized Intake processes the application and releases the application to the Area
               Office after financial eligibility is determined. Medicaid Category 55 may have
               another Medicaid number, the Area Office will need to search by SSN or Medicaid
               number in MMIS and/or contact the EEMS Eligibility worker for resolution, as this
               may affect provider billing.

        Note: An applicant with an SSI category will not have an Eligibility Workflow in the
              Phoenix record.

     04.21   Verification of Medicaid Financial Eligibility

             An applicant's Medicaid financial eligibility must be verified by the SCDHHS
             worker before enrollment in the waiver and by the Case Manager monthly
             thereafter.

             Narrative documentation of a participant’s eligibility verification by the Assigned
             Worker is required initially and monthly, including at quarterly visits and re-
             evaluation via the checklist in the Phoenix narrative.

             The “Financial Eligibility Verified” field, located under Participant Information in
             Phoenix, is used to indicate that the applicant meets all financial criteria. For
             applicants with an eligibility category of 80, 16, 54, 86, 17, 18, 19, 33 and 10, 15, the
             field should be checked in Phoenix. The “financial eligibility verified” field will be
             checked by Centralized Intake. In cases where this has not occurred, the field box
             will be checked by the Area Office worker.

             Note: All other eligibility categories require an application approval notification in
                    the Eligibility Workflow section of Phoenix from the Medicaid Eligibility
                    Long Term Care Worker before the verification field is checked. When the
                    approval notification is received, the designated Area Office staff must
                    check the field. If at any point the applicant becomes financially ineligible,
                    immediate investigation is required to determine what action is necessary or
                    if termination is appropriate. (Refer to Section 5.39.10)

             If a case is released to the CLTC Area Office which does not have an Eligibility
             Workflow and an Eligibility Workflow is required, a Phoenix problem should be
             reported.

             The applicant's Medicaid eligibility status must be verified by one of the following
             methods each month:

             1.      Verification from MMIS (via MMIS or related Phoenix link).

             2.      Verification from Medicaid Eligibility via the Eligibility Workflow section in
                     Phoenix.

             3.      If Medicaid Eligibility Office determines an applicant is financially ineligible,
                     the applicant must not be enrolled into the CLTC program. If a participant is
                     currently enrolled in the CLTC program/waiver, follow policy outlined in
                     5.39.10.

     04.22       Transfer of Resources

                  Federal regulations require Medicaid Eligibility to review the case of any Medicaid
                  applicant or recipient who enters a nursing home or a home and community- based
                  waiver to determine if any transfer of resources has occurred.

             1.        MAO (Category 15) – Resource verification is completed by Medicaid
                       Eligibility as a normal part of the eligibility determination process, prior to
                       providing approval notification in Eligibility Workflow section of Phoenix.
                       No further action concerning transfer of resources is required of the
                       SCDHHS worker.

             2.        All Other Categories of Medicaid Eligibility EXCEPT SSI (Category 80, 86,
                       16, 54, 17, 18, 19 and 10 and 33 and MAO (Category 15). The resource
                       verification must be completed by Medicaid Eligibility prior to approving the
                       Eligibility Workflow in Phoenix. A response from Medicaid Eligibility is required
                       before entering the applicant into the waiver.

             The Centralized Intake Program Assistant:

             •         Determines if the applicant is currently Medicaid financially eligible.

             •         Creates a Medicaid Eligibility Workflow as appropriate.

             •         Provides Medicaid Eligibility application for Nursing Home, Residential or
                       In-Home Form 3401 or Form 3400 B.

             •         Receives notification from Medicaid Eligibility via the Eligibility Workflow
                       that the application has been approved, checks the Medicaid Eligibility
                       Verified Box in Phoenix, and releases the case to the Area Office.

             •         Receives notification from Medicaid Eligibility via the Eligibility Workflow
                       that the application has been denied, terminates the waiver application, and
                       notifies the applicant/Authorized Representative via the CLTC Notification
                       Form 171.

             •         Mails all case correspondence as needed

         04.22.10    Applicant Leaving Nursing Home and Entering Waiver

                     For an individual who is in a nursing facility under Medicaid sponsorship
                     (Category 10 or SSI) and is discharged to a home and community-based
                     waiver, a look back for transfer of resources prior to enrollment in a waiver is
                     not required.

                     MAO (Category 10) applicants who have met the thirty (30) consecutive
                     calendar day criteria, meet all waiver criteria, and wish to enter the waiver,
                     do not have to meet the thirty (30) consecutive calendar day criteria again.

     04.23    Institutionalization of Waiver Participant for Full Calendar Month

             If a waiver participant is institutionalized for a planned, temporary stay, the case must
             be monitored to ensure that the stay does not exceed a full calendar month. If a
             participant remains in the hospital, nursing home, rehabilitation, or any consistent
             combination thereof for a full calendar month, the application should be terminated.
             Refer to Section 05.40 for more information. For institutionalization of a waiver
             participant in a hospice house, see section 5.50.

             If a previous waiver participant is discharged to a home and community-based waiver
             and remains Medicaid Payment Category MAO (Category 15), another look back for
             transfer of resources prior to re-enrollment is not required.

     04.24    Medicaid Income Trusts

             Medicaid Income Trusts allow an individual whose income exceeds the MAO limit to
             become Medicaid eligible for a nursing home (Category 10) or home and community-
             based waiver (Category 15) if the individual so chooses. All eligibility criteria,
             including approval of the trusts by SCDHHS Medicaid Eligibility, must be met
             before an applicant can be certified as Medicaid eligible.

             For all questions regarding Medicaid Income Trusts, refer the applicant and/or
             Authorized Representative to Medicaid Eligibility.

     04.25    Waiver Interaction with Medicaid Managed Care (MCO)

             As part of the Medicaid verification process for waiver enrollment, it must be
             determined through RSP that the recipient is not participating in any other special
             programs. If the RSP indicates an applicant is currently enrolled in an incompatible
             managed care plan or other waiver, the applicant/Authorized Representative must
             indicate which plan or waiver is chosen for participation. This decision should be made
             after all other aspects of waiver eligibility have been determined but prior to
             enrollment. If no response from the applicant/Authorized Representive after 5
             business days, a 171 Notification Form will be sent to inform that the case will be
             closed in 10 days from the date of notification. Phoenix will not allow waiver
             enrollment until the RSP change is reflected in the RSP section under the
             Participant’s Dashboard-General Information tab.

             The SCDHHS worker will provide the choice information to the Managed Care
             Division via the scdhhs.gov agency website. Once on the website, select “For
             Providers” then “Managed Care”, then “Reference Tools”, then “Waiver/Pace
             Enrollment”. Complete and submit the information to the Managed Care Division.
             The information will be reviewed and the managed care staff may contact the
             submitter if information is incorrect. If the information is correct, the disenrollment
             will be retroactive to the end of the month preceding the effective date of waiver

     04.26    Waiver Interaction with Medicare – Medicaid Plan (PRIME)

             Medicare – Medicaid Plan (PRIME) participants may participate in the waiver and
             are subject to the same Medicaid financial eligibility rules and CLTC Policies and
             Procedures as other waiver applicants.

     04.27    Waiver Interaction with Medicaid Targeted Case Management

             If an applicant is currently enrolled in Medicaid Targeted Case Management, the
             applicant and/or Authorized Representative must indicate which plan or waiver is
             chosen for participation.

             This decision should be made after all other aspects of waiver eligibility have been
             determined but prior to enrollment.

             The SCDHHS worker will inform the applicant and/or Authorized Representative of
             available CLTC Waiver Services. The SCDHHS worker will direct the applicant
             and/or Authorized Representative to the assigned Medicaid Targeted Case Manager
             for any questions regarding Targeted Case Management services.

             The assigned SCDHHS worker will mail the Freedom of Choice Form to the
             applicant to use to indicate the choice of programs. The applicant will indicate on the
             form the choice to enter the CLTC waiver or to remain in the Medicaid Targeted Case
             Management program. The signed form will be returned to: Division of Long Term
             Care Transformation, Healthy Connections Medicaid, Post Office Box 8206,
             Columbia, South Carolina 29201-8206

             If the applicant chooses CLTC waiver services, the SCDHHS worker will
             coordinate case transition with the assigned Medicaid Target Case Manager.

             The applicant may be enrolled in the CLTC waiver the calendar day following
             Medicaid Targeted Case Management termination.

             If the applicant chooses not to enroll in the CLTC waiver, the application will be
             terminated as declines participation.

             The discussion of the applicant and/or Authorized Representative choice and the case
             coordination activities with the Medicaid Targeted Case Manager will be
             documented in the narrative record.

         04.28.10    Authorized Representative Agreement

                     Medicaid Eligibility’s Authorized Representative Agreement SCDDHS Form
                     1282 allows an applicant to designate an Authorized Representative and gives
                     a trusted person permission to act on behalf of the applicant during the
                     Medicaid Eligibility application, appeals, review or managed care process.
                     The DHHS Form 1282 must be signed by the applicant/beneficiary or a legal
                     representative of the applicant/beneficiary to be valid. These documents
                     should be faxed to SCDHHS 1-803-255-8209 or mailed to SCDHHS –
                     Community Long Term Care 7th Floor, Post Office Box 8206, Columbia,
                     South Carolina 29202.
