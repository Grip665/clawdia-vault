08.10 Introduction

       Individuals who will enter or remain in a nursing home under primary Medicaid payment
       must meet the South Carolina Medicaid Nursing Facility Level of Care criteria. This level of
       care (LOC) determination is made through pre-admission review by a Community Long
       Term Care, (CLTC), SCDHHS worker. The Level of Care Certification, SCDHHS Form
       185, certifies medical necessity.

       (See Chapter 9 for other necessary actions relative to PASRR.)

       This policy applies to all ages.

08.20 Nursing Home Certification

       CLTC has been designated to perform pre-admission review of all applicants for Medicaid-
       sponsored nursing home care. This function must occur prior to admission to a long term
       care facility and before the date for which Medicaid vendor payment can begin. This
       certification includes the designation of an appropriate level of care. Level of care
       certification is necessary only when an applicant is determined to be at a skilled or
       intermediate level of care and will enter or remain in a nursing home with Medicaid as the
       primary payment source. Level of care certification is accomplished by completing a
       Certification Letter, SCDHHS Form 185.

08.30 When CLTC Certification is Necessary

       Any application for Medicaid-sponsored nursing home care requires two eligibility
       determinations: financial and medical. Financial eligibility is determined by Medicaid
       Eligibility. The authorization of medical necessity, or pre-admission review, is a function
       of CLTC.

       CLTC certification is required in the following situations:

              1. Prior to any Medicaid-sponsored admission to a long term care facility from
                 any location;

                  Note: The only exception to this is if the admission is a transfer of a current
                         Medicaid recipient from another long term care facility at the same
                         level of care.

                          When an applicant is transferring from a Phoenix nursing facility to
                          a non-Phoenix nursing facility, the transferring nursing facility will
                          provide the Level of Care Certification Letter to the accepting non-
                          Phoenix nursing facility.

                          When the applicant is transferring from a non-Phoenix nursing
                          facility to a Phoenix nursing facility, The Phoenix nursing facility
                          that is accepting the applicant will make an electronic nursing home
                          referral. The nursing facility will use the data field on the referral to
                          indicate this case is a transfer from a non-Phoenix nursing facility.

                           Phoenix will create an application and will create an Eligibility
                           Workflow.

                          The accepting nursing facility will report a problem in Phoenix and
                          request the applicant’s level of care and recurring income be entered
                          into Phoenix.

                          A copy of the Level of Care Certification letter will be provided by the
                          transferring nursing facility if the applicant is still under the original
                          Level of Care Certification.


              2. Prior to re-admission to a long term care facility from any location once
                 Medicaid vendor payment has been terminated.

                  a. Re-admission from the hospital if the stay has exceeded a Medicaid ten (10)
                     calendar day bed hold period. (A patient may be in the hospital 10 full
                     calendar days, returning on the 11th calendar day, without need for re-
                     certification.)

                  b. Re-admission to a nursing facility when a patient exceeds nine (9) calendar
                     days of the yearly allowable eighteen (18) calendar days of therapeutic leave
                     or de-institutionalization leave of absence; the calendar day the applicant
                     leaves is counted as Day 1 and the calendar day of return does not count. If
                     the applicant returns after the tenth (10th) calendar day, a new certification

                  c. Re-admission to a nursing facility when a patient exceeds the approved

                 a long term care facility is being paid for privately or by Medicare or any other

                 continue.

              5. Prior to the admission of a patient from a facility administered by the
                 Department of Mental Health (DMH) to a non-DMH administered long term
                 care facility.

              6. Prior to the admission of an Intermediate Care Facility patient from a facility
                 administered by the Department of Disabilities and Special Needs (DDSN) to a
                 non-DDSN long term care facility.

              7. As requested by the Medicaid Eligibility for income trust and transfer penalty
                 case.


       08.31 Certification of Medicare Eligible Applicants

              Prior to nursing home admission, applicants who appear potentially eligible for
              extended care benefits through Medicare should be identified. The facility (hospital
              or nursing home) should be instructed to pursue Medicare reimbursement. A level
              of care is not required, and a Level of Care Certification is not issued for these
              cases.

              If a Nursing Home Application is made by a hospital for an applicant but the
              applicant enters a Phoenix nursing facility under primary Medicare payment, the

              action on the application. The CLTC SCDHHS worker may remove his/her name

              The Phoenix nursing facility will attach themselves to the applicant’s nursing home

       08.32 Skilled Nursing Facility Co-payment

              If an applicant is eligible for Medicare and Medicaid and is under Medicare
              sponsorship in a nursing facility, a certification letter is not required for a hospital
              bed hold period. When Medicare benefits are terminated, the applicant may be
              referred to CLTC for evaluation of Medicaid sponsorship.

              Phoenix Nursing Facilities
              When a nursing home resident receiving Medicaid Skilled Nursing Facility Co-
              Payment is added to a Phoenix nursing facilities roster, the application will appear
              in the CLTC Area Office’s unassigned workflows. The CLTC Area Office must
              determine whether a nursing facility LOC certification is needed at the time of
              referral or if the applicant is receiving Medicaid co-pay only. When making
              referrals, the nursing facility should indicate if the person is receiving Skilled
              Nursing Facility Medicaid co-payment (SNF co-pay). When the facility indicates
              this, the Centralized Intake worker will notate this in the narrative. If the applicant
              is receiving Skilled Nursing Facility Medicaid co-payment (SNF co-pay) only and
              a Nursing Home Conversion is not needed at this time, the case must be assigned
              to a worker and then unassigned for the case to be removed from the “Unassigned
              Centralized Intake Workflows”. If a Skilled Nursing Facility Medicaid co-payment
              (SNF co-pay) case is assigned in error, the SCDHHS worker may remove his/er
              name from the application and document the reason in the narrative.

                   Note: For an applicant receiving Skilled Nursing Facility Medicaid co-
                         payment (SNF co-pay), the Phoenix nursing facilities should enter
                         the anticipated date of conversion to Medicaid primary payment in
                         the participant’s record, even if that date is two-three months away.

              An applicant's medical condition or other functional factors may sometimes warrant
              certification and nursing facility admission for a specific time period. In these
              situations, CLTC has determined that the applicant can benefit from temporary
              placement in a long term care facility. The Level of Care Certification will indicate
              the certification is time limited and will specify the certification period.
              If there is a need for continued nursing facility placement at the end of the
              certification period, the nursing facility must submit a South Carolina Long Term
              Care Assessment Form 1718, signed CLTC Consent Form, and 14 calendar days
              of supporting medical documentation to the CLTC Area Office for level of care
              evaluation at least seven (7) business days before the certification expiration date.

              If the CLTC SCDHHS worker determines that an applicant meets the skilled or
              intermediate level of care, a Level of Care Certification Letter, SCDHHS Form 185,
              will be issued with no time limit.

              When certifying an applicant who will not immediately enter a nursing facility but
              will receive a time-limited certification, the normal thirty (30) calendar day
              expiration date on the Level of Care Certification, DHHS Form 185, should be
              indicated and marked along with the time-limited certification block. At the time
              of admission to the nursing facility, a new Level of Care Certification, DHHS
              Form 185, should be issued indicating the actual time period covered by the time-
              limited certification.

              If there is a need for a change in level of care during a time-limited certification
              period, the nursing facility must forward a South Carolina Long Term Care
              Assessment Form 1718 and a signed CLTC Consent Form to the CLTC Area
              Office.


       08.34 Medicaid Managed Care Organization Plan Members

              For a Medicaid Managed Care Organization (MCO) plan member, the Medicaid
              requirements of participation for nursing facilities apply, including level of care
              certification, preadmission screening resident review (PASRR), resident
              assessment, patient’s rights, etc.

              The MCO must obtain a level of care certification from CLTC for a Medicaid
              MCO program plan member prior to admission to the facility under primary
              Medicaid payment.

              CLTC certification is not required for Medicaid Skilled Nursing Facility Co-
              payment (SNF-Co) admissions.

              The Level of Care Certification will have an effective period of forty-five (45)
              calendar days.

08.40 Certification Validity

       Certification for a person awaiting nursing facility placement is valid for thirty (30)
       calendar days. The thirty (30) calendar day period begins with the effective date on the

Chapter 8-Nursing Home                                                                   Page 9
   SCDHHS
   Community Choices Policy and Procedure Manual
   Chapter 8 – Nursing Home Procedures                                              November 1, 2022


       Level of Care Certification, SCDHHS Form 185, and ends with the expiration date.

       Note: A Level of Care Certification for a Medicaid MCO enrollee has an effective period
             of forty-five (45) calendar days.

       The expiration date is a valid date of certification for the applicant to enter the nursing
       facility. If the applicant has not entered the nursing facility by the expiration date on the
       Level of Care Certification, the applicant's condition must be re-evaluated. (08.51.20 Re-
       Certification of Non-Waiver Applicant) Upon admission to a long term care facility,
       certification is valid indefinitely unless:

          1.   Medicaid benefits are terminated for any reason;
               Exception: A Medicaid recipient's benefits are terminated for thirty-one (31)
               calendar days or less for financial eligibility reasons (e.g., excess resources).

          2.   CLTC has specified that the certification is time-limited (See Section 08.33 Time-
               Limited Certifications).

          3.   Applicant enters a nursing facility with a payment source other than Medicaid; the
               applicant must again be certified before a Medicaid conversion will be allowed.

          4.   The applicant's condition or location from where the applicant was initially certified
               changes (e.g., home to hospital or long term care facility to home); the only
               exception to this rule would be the nursing home's option to hold a bed for up to
               ten (10) calendar days for an applicant who has been transferred to a hospital. If the
               applicant returns to the nursing home on the eleventh (11th) calendar day or
               before, certification by CLTC is not necessary.

       A Level of Care Certification, SCDHHS Form 185, must not be altered by anyone. In
       cases where an error has been made, the Level of Care Certification must be destroyed in
       accordance with HIPAA regulations and a corrected Level of Certification letter issued.
       The new corrected Level of Care Certification letter must have “Corrected Copy” written
       in red ink. Any Level of Care Certification Letter which has been altered is invalid.

       08.41 Facilities Under Denial of Payment for New Admissions

               CLTC will be notified when a nursing facility is placed under Denial of Payment
               for New Admissions (DPNA).

               New admissions on or after the DPNA date will not be certified.

                      •   If the applicant, previously certified in the community, wishes to enter
                          a nursing facility that is under denial of payment, the SCDHHS worker
                          will advise the applicant to speak with the nursing facility.

       08.53 Certification of a Waiver Participant

              If a waiver participant changes the service choice to nursing home and nursing
              home placement is imminent, the Case Manager will:

              •       Complete an electronic referral via Centralized Intake for Pre-admission
                      Screening for Nursing Home Placement.

              •       Continue case management, waiver services and monitoring as required by
                      the waiver.


              When the application is assigned for assessment, the SCDHHS worker will:

               •      Complete a new assessment with the applicant or primary contact
                      Authorized Representative by phone or visit.

               •      Review signed CLTC Consent Form on file.

               •      Complete/ PASRR (PASRR Policy and Procedure in Chapter 9 must be
                      followed.)

               •      Team conference with another SCDHHS worker to determine level of
                      care. The level of care and the justification for the level of care
                      determination must be properly documented by the SCDHHS worker in the
                      Phoenix narrative.

       08.54 Certification for a participant of the Program for All Inclusive Care for the
             Elderly (PACE)

              A referral is not needed for nursing facility level of care certification for PACE
              participants. If a new referral for nursing facility level of care (LOC)
              certification is received for a PACE participant, the application would be
              terminated by Centralized Intake as “Inappropriate After Intake.” If Centralized
              Intake releases the case to the CLTC Area Office, the CLTC Area Office may
              terminate the application.

           08.55.10 Applicant Residing in Out-of-State Nursing Home or Hospital

                          In response to a referral for nursing home placement for an applicant
                          residing in an out-of-state nursing home or hospital, the SCDHHS
                          worker will:

                          •      Send the hard copy South Carolina Long Term Care Assessment
                                 Form 1718, the hard copy CLTC Consent Form and the hard
                                 copy PASRR form to the facility to complete and return to the
                                 CLTC Area Office.

                          •      PASRR Policy and Procedure in Chapter 9 must be followed for
                                 out-of-state applications.

                          •      Instruct a professional staff member of the transferring facility
                                 to obtain written consent and to complete and return the forms
                                 to CLTC for review.

                          •      Contact the out-of-state facility by telephone upon receipt of the
                                 completed forms to validate the information.

                          •      Team conference with another SCDHHS worker to determine
                                 the tentative level of care.

                          •      Document the team conference and tentative level of care in the
                                 narrative record.

                          •      Create and send a CLTC Notification Form to the applicant
                                 and/or primary contact Authorized Representative out-of-state
                                 facility, and the admitting facility to communicate the tentative
                                 level of care.

                          Note: Do not include the Appeal Notice with this Notification because
                          this is a tentative level of care.

                          The case must be reviewed in sixty (60) calendar days to determine case
                          resolution. If upon review, the applicant does not plan to enter a nursing
                          facility in the immediate future, the case may be terminated as “Did Not
                          Relocate to South Carolina.” If the case is closed for this reason, a
                          CLTC Notification Form is sent.

       08.61 Administrative Days

              An applicant may be eligible for Administrative Days if the following criteria is
              met:

              •       Is Medicaid eligible (regardless of age),

              •       Is residing in an acute care hospital,

              •       Has received acute care services,

              •       Is no longer requiring acute care,

              •       Has exhausted other payment sources,

              •       Meets (or tentatively meets) the South Carolina Medicaid nursing facility
                      Level of Care Criteria as determined by CLTC and

              •       Has a nursing home location choice.


              The SCDHHS worker will relay the tentative level of care using the CLTC
              Notification Form.

              This Notification Form will be used by the hospital for billing Administrative Days.

              The use of the tentative level of care is reserved for those applicants who are
              expected to be admitted to a nursing facility within fourteen (14) calendar days.
              The application will remain open pending hospital discharge.

              If the stay exceeds fourteen (14) calendar days, the final level of care will be
              determined by the SCDHHS worker and a Level of Care Certification, DHHS
              Form 185, will be issued.

              The effective date and expiration date on the Level of Care Certification should not
              be completed for Administration Days certification.

              Once a Level of Care Certification Letter is issued, the application is terminated as
              "Entered Administrative Days."

       08.64 Hospice Enrolled Applicants Seeking Nursing Home Certification
             If an applicant receives a Medicare qualifying skilled service for a condition
             unrelated to the terminal diagnosis, Medicare will pay the nursing facility and the
             hospice benefit.

              If an applicant receives a Medicare qualifying skilled service for a condition related
              to the terminal diagnosis, Medicare will only pay the hospice benefit. In this
