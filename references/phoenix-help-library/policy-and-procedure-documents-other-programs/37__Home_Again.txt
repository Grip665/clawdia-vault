01.10 INTRODUCTION

The Home Again program is designed to help individuals who reside in nursing facilities return to their
homes and communities as part of the Money Follows the Person Rebalancing initiative, funded through
the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services. The South Carolina Department of Health and Human
Services started to implement the program in January, 2013 and has assisted 18 individuals to return to
their homes and communities as of December 31, 2013.

Home Again participants are followed for 365 days from the date of Nursing Facility discharge. Home
Again participants are eligible for Community Long Term Care (CLTC) Waiver services in addition to
Home Again specific services which include Transition Coordination and Expanded Goods and Services.
Waiver services will be available to continue to support eligible individuals to remain in the community
after the 365 days as long as the individual meets Waiver eligibility.

This policy contains information specific to the Home Again Program. Otherwise, policy is to be followed
as outlined in the CLTC Policy and Procedure Manual.

01.20 POLICIES AND PROCEDURES

      01.21 Referral

             Follow Policy as outlined in the CLTC Policy and Procedure Manual.

      01.22 Centralized Intake

             Follow Policy as outlined in the CLTC Policy and Procedure Manual. All of the following
             requirements must be met in order for a referral to be considered appropriate. To be
             eligible for the program, an applicant must:

                Currently reside in a skilled nursing facility
                Have been in the skilled nursing facility for at least 90 consecutive days
                Be on a Medicaid payment for at least one day prior to discharge
                Meet nursing facility Level of Care, either intermediate or skilled

             NOTE: A person cannot count Skilled Rehabilitation Services via their Medicare Part A
             benefit as part of the 90 day requirement. The person can count hospital stays as part
             of the 90 days but the person should be re-admitted into the nursing facility at the time
             of transition. The last day of nursing facility payment should be with Medicaid.

      01.23 Eligibility Packet

             Once the referral is processed through Intake, Home Again staff will send the Home
             Again Eligibility Packet to the nursing facility. The nursing facility should return the
             completed packet information within 14 calendar days by fax at 803-255-8209.

             If Home Again staff does not receive the packet within 14 calendar days, the application
             will be terminated. Home Again worker will send out Community Long Term Care
             Notification Form to the referral source, participant and/or primary contact if the
             application is closed.

             Once an applicant meets the eligibility requirements of the Home Again program, Home
             Again staff determines a priority for the case, either 1) regular case or 2) expedited case,
             and transfers the case to the appropriate Community Long Term Care Area Office.

        01.24.20 Consent

                   The Consent Form must be signed by the applicant or a primary contact.

                   NOTE: If the participant is under custody of DSS, the DSS case worker may
                   sign the form.

        01.24.30 Locus

                   The CLTC Participant Service Choice should be obtained indicating the
                   applicant’s desire regarding program participation, and their choice of Locus.
                   If an applicant is residing out-of-state and intends to move to South Carolina,
                   the CLTC Participant Service Choice may be done verbally.

                   NOTE: If the applicant is under custody of DSS, the DSS case worker may sign
                   the Form.

        01.24.40 Assessment

                   The Assessment will be completed by the nurse consultant. The Area Office
                   assigns the application to a nurse consultant. Timeliness standard for Level
                   of Care determination is five calendar days upon the receipt of a completed
                   Assessment.

                   NOTE: If a case is determined as an expedited case, timeliness standard for
                   Level of Care determination is three calendar days upon the receipt of a
                   completed Assessment.

                   During the visit, the nurse consultant obtains the choice of provider for
                   Transition Coordination Visit. The nurse consultant completes the
                   Assessment Section and Medication Section in Phoenix. The nurse
                   consultant does NOT complete the Home Assessment Section, Caregiver
                   Support Section or Non-Waiver Supports Section.

                   The Assessment and Level of Care date MUST be within 30 days of entry into
                   the program. If the Assessment or Level of Care is older than 30 days, a new
                   Assessment must be completed and a Level of Care must be determined and
                   entered into Phoenix. This re-assessment may be completed by phone. If it
                   appears the applicant is medically ineligible, a visit is required.

01.25 Medical Eligibility Criteria

        The Home Again program utilizes nursing facility Level of Care. The applicant must meet
        the established Level of Care which is skilled or intermediate nursing facility Level of
        Care.

        If the applicant is determined to be medically ineligible after receiving physician’s input,
        the nurse consultant will close the application and mail CLTC notification form 171 along
        with the appeals Notification.

        The nurse consultant will notify Home Again staff at homeagain@scdhhs.gov if the

01.26 Financial Eligibility

        Financial eligibility (Medicaid) must be established before a participant can be admitted
        to the program. Follow Policy as outlined in the CLTC Policy and Procedure Manual to
        establish/verify Medicaid eligibility.

01.27 Case Management

        If the participant is determined to be medically and financially eligible, the nurse
        consultant discusses and transfers the application to the case manager II. The case
        manager II should make a referral for Transition Coordination Visit and notifies the
        providers of the referral by call or email. When the provider accepts the referral, the
        case manager II should list the provider as Transition Coordinator in the Home Again
        Application and complete the Transition Coordination Visit authorization, the rate is $0.

        The case manager II should NOT make a referral or authorization for Transition
        Coordination. This service is the payment to transition coordinator providers and will be
        handled exclusively by Home Again staff.

       01.27.10     Table 1540

                   Home Again program utilizes Table 1540 rather than Recipient Special
                   Program (RSP) to pay Home Again claims. The case manager II should email
                   Home Again staff at homeagain@scdhhs.gov and include the following
                   information within three calendar days upon Level of Care determination:

                      Participant’s Name
                      Medicaid Number
                      Home Again Start Date
                      Home Again End Date

                   The Home Again Start Date is the date of the Level of Care determination.
                   The Home Again End date is the 365th day after the participant is discharged
                   from the nursing facility. If the Area Office does not know when the
                   participant will be discharged from the facility, the case manager II may use
                   the 365th day from the Start Date.

                   The case manager II is also responsible to notify Home Again staff once the
                   applicant is discharged from the facility and/or if the application is
                   terminated to update the End Date.

01.28 Pre-transition Planning

       The transition coordinator makes initial visit to the nursing facility to confirm the
       discharge date and complete Risk Assessment within 10 calendar days.

       NOTE: If the case is determined as an expedited case, the transition coordinator
       completes the process within three calendar days.

       If the Home Again Assessment deems the situation inappropriate or unsafe, the
       transition coordinator terminates the Transition Coordination Visit authorization and
       the Home Again application. A Notification is sent to the referral source, participant
       and/or primary contact. The Home Again staff and the case manager II will also be
       notified.

       If the transition coordinator determines the transition to be appropriate, the transition
       coordinator follows and completes the Transition Checklist under the Home Again
       carousel in Phoenix. Once housing in the community has been identified, the transition
       coordinator conducts a Quality of Life survey and home visit to assess the participant’s
       needs.

01.29 Service Plan

       If the Home Again Assessment is deemed as appropriate, the transition coordinator
       notifies the case manager II. The case manager II completes Service Plan within seven
       calendar days of the notification. The Service Plan should be completed prior to the
       transition and while the participant continues to be on the waiting list.

       NOTE: If the case is determined as an expedited case, the case manager II completes
       Service Plan within two calendar days of the notification.

       As part of the Medicaid verification process for program enrollment, it must be
       determined through RSP that the recipient is not participating in any other special
       programs. If RSP indicates a managed care program that does not overlap with CLTC
       program, the case manager II should inform the participant and/or primary contact. The
       participant and/or primary contact will decide whether or not to discontinue the
       managed care program in order to participate in the Home Again program. The case
       manager II will notify designated state worker to dis-enroll the participant from the
       managed care program, if applicable.

01.30 Service Authorization

       The case manager II obtains provider choices and completes referrals and authorizations
       for Waiver services within five calendar days once the Service Plan is completed. The
       case manager II calls potential providers to accelerate the process, if necessary. The
       earliest the authorization start date should be is the date of discharge.

01.31 Enrolling into Home Again

       Once the discharge occurs, case manager II moves the participant to the participating
       status in Phoenix, effective date must be the discharge date, and transfers the case to
       the case management provider. No RSP is entered in MMIS. The case manager II also
       notifies Eligibility worker to change the Medicaid pay category by Eligibility Workflow in
       Phoenix.

01.32 Monitoring

       The case management provider completes the Initial Visit within 30 days of enrollment.
       During the initial visit, the case management provider completes the Home Assessment
       and Caregiver Supports sections in Phoenix. Follow Policy as outlined in the CLTC Policy
       and Procedure Manual for on-going case management.

01.33 Termination

       Follow Policy as outlined in the CLTC Policy and Procedure Manual for termination.

01.34 Transferring after 365 days

       Three weeks prior to the Home Again End Date, the Transition Coordinator creates a
       new referral for CLTC Wavier and sends an email notification of the referral, including
       the Home Again End Date, to the Area Administrator. The transition coordinator will
       copy Home Again staff and Central Office Policy Liaison in the email. The Transition
       Coordinator also completes Quality of Life survey.

       Centralized Intake nurse completes phone Assessment and transfers the case to the
       appropriate Area Office.

       Area Office assigns a nurse consultant. The nurse consultant completes the re-
       evaluation within 14 calendar days from the assignment. The assigned nurse consultant
       may complete the CLTC Assessment by phone if the last Assessment was completed
       within 180 days. An in-home visit is required if the Assessment is over 180 days old or if
       it appears the applicant is medically ineligible. New Consent and Locus choice forms
       should be obtained either verbally or during home visit; however, new provider choice
       forms are not required.

       The nurse consultant transfers the case to case manager II.

       The transition coordinator terminates the Transition Coordination Visit authorization
       and the Home Again application on the Home Again End Date. Authorizations for
       services which will continue under CLTC Waiver will remain active, except Case
       Management Contact and Case Management Visit authorizations. The transition

       The case manager II will create new authorizations for CMC and CMV for the same

       the day following the Home Again End Date. For example, if the person terminates

01.35 Out-of-State Referral

       The receiving CLTC Area Office should follow CLTC Policy and Procedure Manual to
       evaluate the applicant who is residing outside of South Carolina but is seeking to be
       relocated to South Carolina via the Home Again program.

       In response to contact from out of state nursing facility, the nurse consultant sends
       Assessment and Consent Form, DHHS Form 121 to the facility to complete and return to
       the Office. The Assessment must be completed by a professional staff member in the
       out-of-state facility and sent to CLTC with the completed Consent Form, DHHS Form
       121, for review.

       The nurse consultant must contact the out-of-state facility by telephone upon receipt of
       the Assessment to validate the information for the tentative Level of Care after team
       conference per policy.

       The applicant and/or primary contact, out-of-state facility, and Home Again staff must
       be informed of the tentative Level of Care on the CLTC Notification, using the comment
       section.

       If the applicant is seeking housing options in South Carolina, the applicant should stay
       on the Home Again waiting list no more than 6 months. If the applicant has been in the
       waiting list more than 6 months, Transition Coordinator will close the application and
       send a CLTC Notification to applicant and/or primary contact, out-of-state facility.

       NOTE: The applicant may stay in the waiting list more than 6 months with Home Again
       staff’s approval.

       Once the applicant meets tentative Level of Care, the nurse consultant will transfer the
       case to case manager II. The transition coordinator will assist the applicant to locate

housing in South Carolina. When housing is identified, the transition coordinator will
notify the case manager II the potential discharge date. The transition coordinator and
the case manager II will discuss Service Plan and service needs. The case manager II will

may contact potential providers to determine availability prior to the discharge date.

The transition coordinator will ensure that all South Carolina Medicaid financial

The final Level of Care must be determined through a visit to the applicant’s residency
within two calendar days, excluding holidays and weekends, of the applicant's

If the applicant does not meet Level of Care, the application/case will be closed in
Phoenix as "medically ineligible". If the applicant meets Level of Care, follow the
procedures in this policy.

If the applicant does meet Level of Care, the case manager II will ensure that the South
Carolina Medicaid number is entered into Phoenix and MMIS. The case manager II will
start referral process within two calendar days, excluding holidays and weekends, from
the time that Medicaid eligibility determined.

Questions and Answers regarding Home Again Policies and Procedures:

   1. How do we know if an applicant meets the preliminary requirements of the program?
      Once a referral is made, Home Again staff contacts and asks the nursing facility to fill out the
      Home Again Eligibility Packet. And the Home Again staff uploads the document in “Scans” tab in
      Phoenix. The Home Again staff also completes narrative and sends out an email notification to
      the appropriate Area Office.

   2. How can the case manager II check if the transition coordinator is listed in the Home Again
      application?
      Go to Phoenix > click “Application” tab > click “Home Again” > look for Transition Coordinator
      under “General” tab. If the transition coordinator is not listed, the case manager II should click
      “Edit” from the application and select the transition coordinator from the drop down box. If this
      process has not been done, the transition coordinator will not be able to see the participant in
      their Dashboard in Phoenix.

   3. Can case manager II make a referral/authorization for “Transition Coordination Visit” before
      Service Plan set up?
      Yes, Transition Coordination Visit does not require Service Plan to make the referral and
      authorization. The role of transition coordinator is to help with any discharge planning. In order
      to do that, the provider should start to provide the service before the participant is discharged.
      Go to Phoenix > click “Waiver Supports” tab > click “New Referral” > and then choose Transition
      Coordination Visit.

   4. Should case manager II make a referral/authorization for “Transition Coordination”?
      No, Home Again staff in central office will ensure this part.

   5. We do not have any transition coordinators available in our area. What do we do?
      Home Again Transition Coordination Manager will appear as a provider choice where there are
      no contracted providers for Transition Coordination Visit.

   6. How case manager II ensures that the applicant is in Table 1540?
      The case manager II should email Home Again staff at homeagain@scdhhs.gov with the
      applicant’s name, Medicaid number, Start Date, and End Date.

   7. The case manager II has made a request for addition of the participant to Table 1540 but has not
      received the confirmation email yet. Should case manager II wait to hear back from the point
      person to proceed the next step?
      No, the case manager II should go ahead and proceed with the next step as soon as the request
      has been made.

   8. Can case manager II complete a Service Plain while a participant is on the waiting list?
      Yes, the necessary changes to the Phoenix have been made and the case manager II should
      complete the Service Plan while the participant is on the waiting list.

   9. Nurse consultant transferred the case to the case manager II and the case is not moving forward
      because there is no housing identified. Is it okay to have the Home Again case open for several
      months?

    Yes, it is part of discharge planning and it will take some time to locate housing unless the
    participant has a place to go. The case manager II should NOT get any penalty for having the
    case for a longer period of time.

10. The Home Assessment has not been done because the participant was at nursing facility when
    the nurse consultant went out to see the person. Who is responsible to complete the Home
    Assessment?
    Case management provider is responsible for Home Assessment.

11. Can a Home Again participant be enrolled with MCO during the first 365 days once they have
    transitioned to the community?
    No, dual-enrollment with MCO is not allowed with the Home Again program at this time. Home
    Again will work with SC Prime for dually eligible participants. Additional policy guidance will be
    issued at a later date.

12. Can a Ramp provider build a ramp before the discharge date?
    Yes, the case manager II should make a referral to Ramp providers through Phoenix once the
    need is identified. The case manager II should call the Ramp provider and explain that the
    reimbursement will occur once the participant is discharged from a nursing facility. The
    authorization should be made prior to the discharge date. The authorization start date should
    be either discharge date or any time after the discharge date.

13. Can case manager II wait to do Service Plan, referrals, and authorizations until a participant is
    discharged?
    No. Service Plan, referrals, and authorizations should be done prior to the discharge date,
    except out of state cases

14. How case manager II start referral and authorization process prior to moving the applicant to
    the participating list?
    Home Again referrals and authorizations can be initiated from the “Waiver Supports” tab. Go to
    Phoenix > click “Waiver Supports” tab > click “New Referral” button.
