                               Personal Care Service
• Effective July 1, 2023, Personal Care I and Personal Care II services will be
  consolidated into Personal Care Service.
  • All tasks provided in Personal Care I and Personal Care II services will be maintained in the new
    Personal Care Service.
  • The rate will be the current Personal Care II rate.

• Phoenix Changes: Phoenix is set to automatically issue new authorizations as of
  July 2, 2023, for the new Personal Care Service.
  • Current combined authorized hours for Personal Care I and Personal Care II will automatically
    be the total number of hours authorized for Personal Care Service.
  • Case managers will re-assess participants during the annual re-evaluation process to
    determine if current service levels remain appropriate and make necessary changes at that
    time.
  • Personal Care I will no longer be available for referral or authorization.
  • Service Plans will be updated to combine Personal Care I and Personal Care II to Personal Care
    Services.

                              Nursing Service

• Medicaid Nursing RN and Medicaid Nursing LPN will be consolidated into Nursing
  Service. The Nursing Service rate will be the current Medicaid Nursing RN rate.
• Enhanced Nursing RN and Enhanced Nursing LPN will be consolidated into
  Enhanced Nursing Services. The Enhanced Nursing rate will be the current
  Enhanced Nursing RN rate.
• Providers will determine whether an RN or LPN is needed based on the assessed
  needs of the participant and what is allowable under the scope of practice
  dictated in SC Nurse Practice Act Title 40 Chapter 33.

                         Primary Caregiver Policy
• For Self-Directed Attendant and Individual Companion Care ONLY, the primary
  caregiver policy will be updated to the following:
   • Attendant may be related to participants served by the agency within limits
     allowed by the South Carolina Family Caregiver Policy. The following family
     members cannot be a paid caregiver:
      A parent of a minor Medicaid participant.
      A stepparent of a minor Medicaid participant.
      A foster parent of a minor Medicaid participant.
      Person who has the legal responsibility of utilizing their own assets for the
        care of the Medicaid participant (legally responsible adults).
• Please note that this does not affect agency services (Personal Care Services,
  Children’s Personal Care Services, Nursing Services, Enhanced Nursing Services,
  Companion Agency Services).


                                                                                       17
                         Primary Caregiver Policy


• Legally responsible adults are still prohibited from providing services.

• Legally responsible adults are defined as those who are court-ordered to utilize
  their assets for the care of Medicaid participants.

• Court documents will have to be reviewed to ensure legal guardians, power of
  attorneys, spouses, etc. have not been given that legal responsibility. A service
  ticket must be submitted by area office staff to request review of paperwork to
  ensure it does not make them a legally responsible adult.




                                                                                      18
                          Primary Caregiver Policy

• Effective July 1, 2023, newly hired personal care aides or nurses will follow the
  existing Primary Caregiver Policy which states:
  • The following family members cannot be a paid caregiver:
      The spouse of a Medicaid participant (including married but separated).
      A parent of a minor Medicaid participant.
      A stepparent of a minor Medicaid participant.
      A foster parent of a minor Medicaid participant.
      Any other legally responsible guardian of a Medicaid participant.

• Legally responsible persons and/or relative/legal guardians, as well as parents of
  minor children who have been granted exceptions due to Appendix K, will receive
  further communication on the change before they will be expected to revert to
  policy.

                                                                                               21
        Actions Required by CMs: Authorized Units

Case managers must ensure that units authorized for the following services do not
exceed prior authorization in the current service plan:
   • Personal Care I
   • Personal Care II
   • Medicaid Nursing RN
   • Medicaid Nursing LPN
   • Enhanced Nursing RN
   • Enhanced Nursing LPN

Central office/area office staff may reach out with a list of participants whose
authorized units exceed prior authorization. Case managers should respond within
one business day of receiving this request.



                                                                                22
Actions Required by CMs: Service Plans/Authorizations

• Prior to 4 p.m., July 1, 2023, CM/CM providers must complete all open service
  plans (in cooperation with CLTC area offices).

• Prior to 4 p.m., July 1, 2023, CM/CM providers must make certain that all
  referrals and authorizations for personal care and nursing services have been
  either accepted or declined.

• For the time period after 4 p.m., July 1 and prior to 8 a.m., July 3, 2023, users
  must not enter any authorizations for old services or new services. Newly
  consolidated services can be authorized after 8 a.m. July 3, 2023.
