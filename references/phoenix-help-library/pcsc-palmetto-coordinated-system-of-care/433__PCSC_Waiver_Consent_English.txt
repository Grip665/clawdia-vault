As part of the application for waiver program services, the applicant must be evaluated by the South
Carolina Palmetto Coordinated System of Care (PCSC) Waiver Program. The PCSC Waiver Program is
operated by the South Carolina Continuum of Care (COC) and administered by the South Carolina
Department of Health and Human Services (SCDHHS), the state agency administering the Medicaid
Program in South Carolina.

By signing this document, I agree to the following:

I understand that eligibility for the PCSC waiver is contingent upon the Child and Adolescent Service
Intensity Instrument (CASII) assessment score, approval of Medicaid eligibility, and consent for waiver
services as indicated with the below signature. The below consent indicates agreement for services
effective the date of the CASII assessment.

I have received a Notice of Privacy Practices pursuant to the Health Insurance Portability And
Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) explaining how SCDHHS obtains my Personal/Protected Health
Information (PHI) and disseminates it with others who may provide services as part of the PCSC Waiver
Program.

I understand my medical and social needs must be evaluated to determine whether I meet program
eligibility requirements. If I am deemed eligible, my care will be coordinated by a Wrap Facilitator
employed by the SC Continuum of Care and monitored by the PCSC Care Coordination Team. The PCSC
Care Coordination Team may include, but not be limited to, the Continuum of Care, and/or a PCSC Waiver
service provider.
I consent to allow the PCSC Care Coordination Team to obtain and share PHI in the following manner:

1.        I consent to the release of PHI by the PCSC Care Coordination Team to authorized family members,
social service professionals, physicians, nurses, other medical personnel, agencies, organizations, or
facilities as long as it pertains to my diagnosis and treatment as part of the PCSC Waiver Program. If I do
not qualify for the PCSC Waiver Program, I consent to the release of PHI to the Continuum of Care. The
Continuum will have you to sign individual Consents to release information outlining the scope of
information shared.

2.      If the PCSC Care Coordination Team believes I may be the victim of abuse, neglect, or exploitation,
I understand that staff are mandated reporters and information may be reported to organizations
authorized to investigate such allegations, including, but not limited to, the Department of Social Services,
state and county law enforcement, and/or the South Carolina Attorney General’s Office. I consent to the
release of PHI by the PCSC Care Coordination Team to these organizations to the extent it would assist in
an investigation.

I have received a copy of this signed Consent.

will not be effective until I provide the PCSC Care Coordination Team with a signed written notification. I
understand amending or revoking this Consent could affect my ability to participate in the PCSC Waiver
Program. This Consent will expire one (1) year after my discharge from the PCSC Waiver Program.
