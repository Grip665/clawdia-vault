Dear Contact Person,

The Palmetto Coordinated System of Care (PCSC) Waiver Administration Team within the SCDHHS
Division of Community Options conducted a random quality assurance review of a small sample
of Year One waiver participant records. The goal of these preliminary reviews is to provide
guidance prior to the annual comprehensive 100% performance measure reviews required to
meet CMS waiver standards. Further, the review process promotes compliance with state
policies/procedures, federal regulations, and federal assurances.

The initial sampling review of PCSC waiver case management practices rendered by your agency has been
completed for the period of August 1, 2019 – July 31, 2020. A preliminary list of identified deficiencies are
attached to this letter.

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:
    1. Recoupment: Waiver Case Management services that were billed but are out of compliance with
        policy and procedures may be recouped from the Provider. Total amount to recoupment based
        on findings: $XXXX.XX.
    2. Plan of Correction: The Waiver Case Management Provider is required to submit a Corrective
        Action Plan outlining the deficiency(ies), the detailed plan to correct the deficiency(ies) and the
        effective date the plan will be implemented.
    3. Training: The Waiver Case Management Provider is required to participate in bi-monthly trainings
        to ensure staff are well-versed in PCSC Operating Entity Roles and Responsibilities, Policy and
        Procedures, and Waiver Case Management Scope of Service.

Please examine the attached findings and contact Janice Bailiff at (803) 898-2043 should additional
clarification be required. Failure to respond to this correspondence within ten (10) calendar days of the
receipt of this correspondence constitutes agreement of the content therein. As a result, we will move
forward with the recommended recoupment and course of action outlined above.
