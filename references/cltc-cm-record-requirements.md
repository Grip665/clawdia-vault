# CLTC / Case-Management — participant record: what's required + update clocks

Built 2026-10-16 (Larry Q: "what forms do we have to have on file as a CM? do any of them get updated?").
**Key point:** the CM side is a DIFFERENT world from the PHC *provider* packet. As a CM, the record lives in **Phoenix**; the provider forms (#02/#03/#04/#07/#11 etc.) do NOT apply to the CM.

## Sources
- SCDHHS HCBS Scope **760 — Case Management Services** (sanctions/recoupment §J)
- Community Choices P&P **Ch. 5 Case Management** (REV content)
- **CLTC Documentation Policy** (Division of Long Term Living, Mar 2019)
- Form **750 — CLTC Service Plan Agreement**
- HIV/AIDS P&P §01.23.20–.30 (Consent + Service Choice)
- PCSC Provider Manual (LOC annual)

## Documents/records the CM must have (per participant)
| Item | Signed by | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Consent Form (SCDHHS Form 121)** | participant / AR (DSS worker if in DSS custody) | at the time of assessment / enrollment |
| **CLTC Participant Service Choice** | participant / AR | at the time of assessment (program + location choice) |
| **Level of Care (LOC)** | team-staffed | established at initial referral, **re-determined ANNUALLY** |
| **Person-Centered Service Plan (PCSP)** | — | **new plan at each re-evaluation (annual)** |
| **CLTC Service Plan Agreement (Form 750)** | **participant/AR + Case Manager** | with each Service Plan → **ANNUAL** ⚠️ *recoup if not signed* |
| **CLTC Participant Rights and Responsibilities Form** | participant/AR | with the intake packet (goes in the CLTC Information Folder) |
| Authorized Representative — **Form 1282 LTL** | participant / AR | reviewed at intake; full form if an AR is appointed |
| CLTC Notification Form (**Form 171**) | CM (notice to participant) | issued as needed (adverse decision, closure, intake notice) — informational, no re-sign clock |
| NS Physician Order Form | physician | as needed for services requiring a physician order |
| Participant Information Form | participant / CM | at intake |
| Assessment / Initial Visit documentation | CM | per intake |
| Monthly Contact | CM | **every month** (≥1 CM activity/mo) |
| Quarterly Visit (QV) | CM | **every 3 months**, in-home |
| Annual Re-evaluation (REV) | CM | **annually**, in-home; incl. LOC re-determination, locus choice, Medicaid financial eligibility |
| Emergency / Disaster plan + priorities | CM/participant | completed; ⚠️ *recoup if not done* |
| Personal goals | CM | discussed/addressed each activity; ⚠️ *recoup if not* |
| Authorizations | CM | initial / changes / terminations |
| Narratives + checklists | CM | for EVERY activity |
| Phoenix Scans | — | anything not in Phoenix, under the correct scan tag |

## ⚙️ How these exist in practice (Larry, 2026-10-16)
On the CM side these are **NOT paper forms you hunt down** — they are **Phoenix dashboard sections / options** you complete. You can also **print a physical copy and scan it in** if you need a hard copy. So treat the list above as **"sections/options to have completed,"** not "PDFs on file." (Applies to the Service Plan Agreement too — it's a dashboard section, not a loose PDF.)

## Update clocks (the only real "expirations")
- **Service Plan Agreement (750) → annual** (new PCSP at each REV).
- **Level of Care → annual** re-determination.
- **Consent Form 121 + Service Choice → per enrollment** (at assessment; again on re-enrollment).
- **Emergency/Disaster plan → on change** (no fixed interval).
- Monthly contact (monthly) · QV (quarterly) · REV (annual) · Medicaid eligibility (verify monthly).
- **Retention: 5 years after last payment** (6 yrs nursing-home docs); hold longer if litigation/audit.

## Documentation timeliness (recoup/strike risk)
- Narrative + checklist for any activity: **within 3 business days** (else strike, then recoup).
- **All** monthly documentation keyed/uploaded by **11:59 pm the last day of the month**.
- External docs (physician orders, packets) scanned by month-end — or by the 5th if received in the last 3 business days.
- Invalid/late Service Plan, unsigned Service Plan agreement, missing goals, missing disaster plan → **recoup**.

## ⚠️ Note (corrected 2026-10-16 from Willie Bunkley 9758825's actual Phoenix Forms list)
- **There IS a CM-side rights form: the CLTC Participant Rights and Responsibilities Form.** (This is *not* the same as the provider's "#03 Right to Complain" — different form, different entity.)
- **No advance directive on the CM side** — AD is a *provider*-side duty (Scope 703 §E.3.a).
- The CLTC **Information Folder** delivered at intake must include: CLTC Brochure · Participant Rights & Responsibilities copy · CM business card · Estate Recovery brochure · Choosing a Case Manager · Choosing an ADHC · Choosing PC I/PC II · APS brochure · Emergency Preparedness info · Emergency Telephone Numbers Form · Evacuation info · Emergency Checklist · Helpful Reminders in an Emergency brochure.
- The CM's participant-facing **signed** docs = Consent 121, Rights & Responsibilities, Participant Service Choice, and the Service Plan Agreement (750).
