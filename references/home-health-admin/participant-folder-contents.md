# Participant / Client Folder — Compliance Contents Checklist (Home Health / Personal Care)
> **Source of authority:** SCDHHS HCBS Provider Manual, Scope of Services — *Personal Care Services, HASCI Attendant, Respite* (Scope 703), **effective July 1, 2025** — sections E (Conduct of Service) + 7 (Record Keeping). SPECIFIC SECTIONS VERIFIED VERBATIM AGAINST THE OFFICIAL PDF.
> **Why it matters:** These are what DPH/Medicaid audits check. Missing items = survey deficiencies + overpayment exposure. Built 08-11-2026.
> Scope PDF: scdhhs.gov → HCBS Provider Manual → Scopes of Service → Personal Care.

## Verified record framework (from the scope, line-by-line)
The scope enumerates **six** active-record contents, but several *additional* docs must also exist in the record per the Conduct-of-Service and administrative sections. Auditors cite both. Groups A–B below.

---

## A. Mandatory contents (active participant record)
### GROUP 1 — Authorization & Eligibility
1. **Service Provision Form / Authorization** — current **AND past** must be printed & in the record (§E.1). Services must NOT begin before the authorized start date — providing without a valid, current auth is the **#1 overpayment trigger**.
2. **Medicaid eligibility verification records** — provider verifies eligibility at acceptance AND **monthly thereafter** (§B.7); keep dated proof (Phoenix dashboard / Medicaid Web Tool).

### GROUP 2 — Care / Service Planning
3. **SCDHHS Service Plan** — current **+ annual** versions maintained in the record (§E.2). *OIDD exception:* no SCDHHS Service Plan — keep the Personal Care/Attendant Care assessment attached to the auth instead.
4. **Provider RN/LPN pre-start home visit** — visits prior to service start; recorded in **EVV** from the home AND documented in the record (§E.3.a). If participant was already receiving a similar service, a NEW initial visit is required before PCS start.
5. **Provider task list** — the RN/LPN-built menu of duties from the Service Plan, developed **prior to service start**, documented in the record. *Distinct from the task sheet* (the recurring daily log) (§E.2 + §E.3.a.i).
6. **Physician orders + RN delegation** — only for **skilled** services (HASCI/MCC): physician order + authorization + RN training/delegation, obtained **prior** to the aide rendering service; docs in BOTH participant file + aide personnel file (§E.7.c). Not applicable to non-skilled companion/personal-care.

### GROUP 3 — Consent, Rights & Administrative
7. **Advance Directives** — at the **initial nurse visit**, give written advance-directive info; the **participant signs AND dates** a receipt statement, and the **nurse supervisor also signs AND dates** (§E.3.a.ii). Enumerated record content (§E.7.e.v.d).
8. **Right to Complain** — inform participant; **both participant AND nurse supervisor sign AND date** (§E.3.a.iii). The acknowledgment MUST contain the name + phone + address of who to complain to; **first point of contact = a provider employee; second = the licensing agency (DPH, LLR, etc.)**. Signed acknowledgment missing the two-tier contact info = a deficiency (§E.3.a.iii, §E.7.e.v.c).
9. **Complaint investigations** — complaints against aides must be investigated and action taken; documentation maintained in **both the participant's and the aide's** file (§E.3.a.iv).
10. **HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP) acknowledgment** — federally required for all clients; provide NPP + good-faith effort to get written acknowledgment of receipt. Authority: **45 CFR §164.520** (NOT in the SCDHHS scope — verify your own HIPAA policy manual's form matches).
11. **Backup-plan documentation** — effective written backup plan; use of the backup plan + any CM/CC telephone calls about missed services recorded **in the participant's file** (§E.9).
12. Standard practice additions: **incident reports**, **medication list**, **consents** as applicable.

**Retention:** active records ≥ **2 years** of documentation; ALL records retained at least **5 years** (Medicaid policy) (§E.7.e.v). Records kept at contract address, available for SCDHHS review (§E.8).

## B. Task sheets / daily logs (HIGH compliance risk — read closely)
- ❌ **Do NOT ask the participant/responsible party to sign** any log or task sheet.
- ✅ Task sheet must be **reviewed, signed (ORIGINAL signature — stamps NOT acceptable), and dated every 2 weeks by the supervisor**.
- ✅ Task sheets filed in the participant's record **within 30 days of service delivery**.
- ✅ If a **resolution is submitted** for a date of service → a task sheet listing services provided **MUST be in the participant's file** for that date.
- ✅ Multiple services (e.g., PCS + Companion) can be on one sheet if each service/task is easily identifiable.
- Office rule (from training): print task sheets **alphabetical**, keep **chronological** in the tabbed tasksheet folder. Missing sheets = serious compliance infraction.

## C. Supervisory visits (Personal Care) — schedule + content
**Schedule:**
- **Initial supervisory visit within 30 days** after service is initiated.
- Then **at least once every 4 months** per participant (must be done by end of 4th month).
- **Aide must be present during ≥1 supervisory visit each 12-month period.**
- **Break in service >60 days** → complete a **new initial visit** when services resume.
- Supervisory visits are documented in the participant record **and** recorded in EVV from the participant's home (SCDHHS).

**Supervisor's on-site visit report must include (at minimum):**
1. Services being delivered consistent with the **Service Plan/Authorization**.
2. Participant's **needs being met**.
3. Reference to any **complaints** lodged.
4. Brief statement on **changes in service needs**.
5. Supervisor's **original signature + date** (stamps NOT acceptable).

**Filing:** all supervisory visit documentation filed in the participant's record **within 30 days of the visit**.

*(Training cross-check: nurse/RN client visit cadence every ~120 days aligns with the 4-month supervisory visit.)*

## D. Office filing structure (per training)
- **2 folders per client:** (1) client folder (no tab), (2) **tabbed** folder for **task sheets**.
- **Authorizations on one side, nurse visits on the other.**
- Folders **alphabetical by last name**. If too thick → split/shift.
- Green **out-card** when pulling a file.

## G. Additional mandatory documentation (Groups 4–6) — all verified §E/§F
**Missed / attempted visit documentation** — for SCDHHS participants, when an authorized daily service isn't delivered (aide couldn't complete OR participant unavailable), record the reason in **EVV**; weekly, indicate reasons by **close of business the following week**. **Two consecutive** attempted/missed visits → notify the local SCDHHS office; document in the record + Phoenix Provider Portal (§E.7.f–g). (Not required for OIDD/HASCI/IDRD/CS/PCSC.)

**Change-of-condition / status notifications to CM** — notify the CM/CC **within 2 working days** of: (i) condition changed / plan no longer meets needs; (ii) institutionalized/dies/moves out of area; (iii) no longer wants services; (iv) Medicaid ineligibility or potential ineligibility; (v) ANE report made to authorities. Document each notification (§E.7.d).

**Abuse/Neglect/Exploitation (ANE) reporting** — mandatory reporter; report + the CM notification stay **in-file** (§E.7.d.v). Failure to report = immediate action up to termination.

**Chronological order** — records (incl. medical) must be kept **in chronological order**; disorganized files are independently citable (§F.7).

**Office availability** — office open/staffed **10:00–4:00 M–F**; available by phone **8:30–5:00**; emergency number outside hours; records at contract address, available for review (§F.8). Sanctions for failure.

## H. EVV, electronic & paperless records
- **Paperless allowed** — changes the medium, not the content: every mandatory doc must still exist in the electronic file; must have a **reliable backup system** (absence of backup = citable) (§F.9 / A&B Manual).
- **Record accessibility (A&B Manual):** records must be **accessible/retrievable immediately**, at the **service address** on the provider-enrollment record, available to SCDHHS/SAO/SCAG/HHS/GAO; a requested record is **"inaccessible" if not produced within 2 hours** of an authorized request. Electronic records accepted per the **Uniform Electronic Transactions Act** (S.C. Code §26-6-10) + HIPAA.
- **Authentication:** every clinical entry signed by its **author** (handwritten/electronic/digital). **Stamped signatures never acceptable** — failure = claim denial (A&B Manual, Signature).
- **EVV is an ADDITIONAL layer, not a replacement** for task sheets: EVV check-out captures the visit electronically; the task sheet (supervisor-reviewed every 2 weeks) documents what was done. You need BOTH.
- **Resolution rule again:** a Phoenix task-sheet report can serve as delivery documentation — but the moment a resolution is filed for a date, a **task sheet must be physically in that participant's file**.
- Signatures: electronic/digital OK; **never stamps**.

## I. Retention — the three clocks (use the longest per doc)
- **HCBS scope (personal-care + companion):** active records ≥ **2 years** of documentation (task sheets, plans, supervisory visits, complaints); **all records ≥ 5 years** "per Medicaid policy" (§E.7.e.v / Companion scope).
- **Admin & Billing Manual (general Medicaid):** fiscal + health records ≥ **4 years after last payment**; hospitals/SNF 6 yrs. If litigation/claim/audit is initiated, retain until resolution or end of period, whichever is later. (A&B Manual, Record Retention.)
- **HIPAA: 6 years** for HIPAA compliance docs (policies, NPP acknowledgments, authorizations) — 45 CFR §164.530(j).
- **Rule: default to the LONGEST of the three per document type** (for these client folders, that's the 5-yr scope floor, and 6 yrs for HIPAA logos/docs).


## J. Companion Care scope — key differences
> **Effective date — RESOLVED 08-12 (corrected after primary-doc review): July 1, 2025.** The hosted file is named "...Final Draft 7-1-25.pdf" and, despite its stale "July 1, 2024" internal footer, the live `7-1-25` document carries a substantive 7/1/2025 change — the family-caregiver exclusion now reads "any other legally responsible adult or legal guardian of a Medicaid participant," a line the official **7/1/2025 HCBS training deck** lists verbatim under Companion scope/standard changes. The footer is a template artifact the drafters didn't refresh; the filename path + body content + training deck all confirm **7/1/2025**.
Companion = **supervision + some IADLs (meal/snack prep, laundry, shopping, sitting) — NO ADLs, NO skilled/medical/hands-on care by the companion** ("under no circumstances... any type of skilled medical service or hands on care").
- **Supervisory cadence DIFFERS from Personal Care:** initial visit **within 90 days** of service start, then **on-site supervision at least once every 365 days** (annually) + phone contact as needed. (NOT PCS's 30-day/4-month.)
- Visit documented in **EVV** in participant's file; participant inaccessible → complete **within 5 working days** of resumption; break in service **>60 days** → supervisor visit **within 90 days** of resumption.
- **Supervisor report = same 4 items** (services-match-plan; needs met; complaints reference; changes-in-needs statement).
- **Right to Complain + Advance Directives:** same double rule (participant AND supervisor both sign+date); complaint form with 2-tier contact (provider employee → licensing agency DPH/LLR); complaints against companions investigated/referred to supervisor.
- **Task list** developed by the **supervisor** (not RN-specific) from the Authorization; provider task list must be in participant file.
- **Task sheets/daily logs:** same — no participant signature; supervisor original sign+date **every 2 weeks**; filed **≤30 days**. OIDD → daily service logs instead.
- **Retention:** active ≥2 yrs, all ≥5 yrs (same); contents same 7 items.
- **2-consecutive missed/attempted** → notify local Area office; missed visits in record + EVV.
- **2-working-day CM notifications** (fewer triggers than PCS: condition change/no longer needs, died/institutionalized/moved, no longer wants services, Medicaid ineligibility).
- **Administrative:** Policy & Procedure Manual required; org chart with lines of authority/names; insurance (liability + workers' comp) listing SCDHHS as certificate holder; SCDHHS P.O. Box 8206, Columbia SC 29202; notify SCDHHS ≤3 working days of key-personnel change; chronological records.

## K. Admin & Billing Manual — cross-checks (provider.scdhhs.gov / scdhhs.gov "Provider Administrative and Billing Guide")
