# SC Home Health Aide Background Checks — Accurate Reference
> For Larry to explain to the owner / anyone. Corrected 2026-08-11 (residency nuance).
> Not legal advice. Confirm current forms with HQCBC@dph.sc.gov; take enforcement questions to a healthcare attorney.

## The short version
It is **residency-based, not "everyone gets fingerprinted."**
- Aide who **verifies 12 months of SC residency** → **SLED state check only** is what the statute requires. No FBI/fingerprints.
- Aide who **cannot verify SC residency** → state check first, then a **federal (fingerprint/FBI) check**.
- Aide who verifies **12 months residency in another state** → may run only that state's check.

So fingerprints (IdentoGO) are required for aides who **can't document SC residency** — not automatically for everyone. (Many agencies make prints universal as policy, and Medicaid waivers/payer contracts sometimes require more — but that's agency/payer policy, not the statute itself.)

## The law
- **S.C. Code § 44-7-2910(A)(1):** a direct care entity (incl. **home health agency**) must conduct a criminal record check **before** employing/contracting a direct caregiver.
- **Fingerprint/FBI requirement** only kicks in when the **federal check** is needed (residency not verified).
- "Direct caregiver" is broad: unlicensed persons providing physical assistance, anyone working in a building housing patients, anyone whose duties include possible patient contact.

## When the federal (fingerprint) check IS needed — the process
1. Agency must have a **valid relationship** with the applicant at time of fingerprinting (SLED directive).
2. Agency completes the **"Verification of Application and Authorization for Background Check"** form → email **HQCBC@dph.sc.gov**.
3. Provide the applicant the **Noncriminal Justice Applicant's Privacy Rights** + **FBI Privacy Act Statement** (mandatory notices).
4. DPH issues a **service code** → aide goes to **IdentoGO** for live-scan → SLED + FBI.
5. Applicant can challenge record accuracy (SLED (803) 737-9000 in-state; FBI CJIS for out-of-state).

## Fee
- ~**$51.75** at IdentoGO (subject to change), payable online when scheduling.
- Either party may pay — per SC AG, the fee "must be paid by the individual direct caregiver or the direct care entity." Making aides pay is a recruiting handicap; most agencies absorb/reimburse.

## What goes in the file
- **NOT the prints** — IdentoGO captures and transmits them; you never possess them.
- **The result / DPH eligibility determination**, retained as received.
- For home health agencies (PL 105-277): DPH can only forward actual results to a rep **vetted by SLED**; if no one is vetted, DPH makes an **eligibility determination** and you keep **that** in the caregiver's file. (Contact HQCBC if you want SLED vetting.)
- Safe practice: file the determination exactly as issued **plus proof of when the check was initiated**. ("Date/time-stamp" phrasing vs Reg 61-77 is unconfirmed — don't over-claim it.)

## Agency-level check (separate — NOT about aides)
- **42 CFR § 455.434 / 455.450:** fingerprint-based check for the **provider or anyone with 5%+ ownership** for **high-risk providers**. Home health agencies are high-risk; they **revalidate every 3 years** (vs 5 for others).
- FCBC became a Healthy Connections enrollment requirement **Aug 15, 2022**.
- So: § 44-7-2910 screens **caregivers**; 42 CFR 455 screens **owners (5%+)** at enrollment + each 3-yr revalidation. Same IdentoGO fee scale, different people/legal basis.
- At revalidation, submitting a copy of the **fingerprint appointment confirmation** avoids termination for missing the 30-day deadline.

## Legacy aides (hired years ago, no prints) — sort each file
- **(a) State check done + residency documented** → likely **compliant**; document residency if it's missing.
- **(b) State check done, residency never verified** → the federal check that should've been commenced wasn't.
- **(c) No check at all** → clear violation (prior-to-employment requirement).

### Corrective action for (b)/(c)
- Run the missing checks **now** and **document everything**: the gap, date discovered, corrective steps, revised hiring procedure.
- **Tenure break ≥ 1 year** with a direct care entity → check must be **repeated** before resuming.
- **Do NOT quietly backfill** — write a **dated corrective-action memo** (best protection).

### Ramifications
- Missing background checks in files = **standard DPH licensing survey deficiency** under **Reg 61-77** → citations + plan of correction; possible monetary penalties/license action if uncorrected or serious.
- If affected aides serve **Medicaid clients**, a payer audit could raise **overpayment** questions.
- Take the (a)/(b)/(c) file sort to a **healthcare regulatory attorney before the next survey/revalidation**.
