🖐️ SC Background Checks — Who Does What

Home health aide hiring compliance · corrected 08-11-2026 · for Larry & owner review · not legal advice

⚠️ Key correction — it's RESIDENCY-BASED, not universal

  • Aide who verifies 12 months of SC residency (SC license/ID, rent/mortgage/utility, pay stubs, or SC bank records) → SLED state check only is lawful. No fingerprints needed.
  • Aide who can't verify SC residency → state check, then a federal fingerprint (FBI) check.
  • Verified 12-mo residency in another state → may run only that state's check.
  • Universal prints is common agency policy (and payers/Medicaid waivers can impose more) — but it's not what § 44-7-2910 itself requires.

TRACK 1 — Per-AIDE (direct caregiver, § 44-7-2910) AIDE

  • Required before employing any direct caregiver (home health agencies are listed "direct care entities").
  • Type depends on residency (see correction above). Fingerprints/IdentoGO only when the federal check is needed.
  • When prints are needed, your steps: agency has a valid relationship → complete the "Verification of Application and Authorization for Background Check" form → email HQCBC@dph.sc.gov → provide the Privacy Rights + FBI Privacy Act Statements (mandatory) → DPH returns a service code → aide goes to IdentoGO (live-scan) → SLED + FBI.
  • Cost: ~$51.75 at IdentoGO (subject to change). Either party may pay (SC AG) — absorbing it is recruiting-friendly.
  • File: the result / DPH eligibility determination, NOT a print card. For home health (PL 105-277), if no SLED-vetted rep, DPH's eligibility determination is your file doc.

TRACK 2 — AGENCY-level (owners, NOT aides) AGENCY

  • 42 CFR § 455.434 / .450: fingerprint-based check on the provider or anyone with 5%+ ownership for high-risk providers — home health agencies are high-risk.
  • Revalidate every 3 years (vs 5 for others). FCBC became a Healthy Connections enrollment requirement Aug 15, 2022.
  • Different people, different law — this screens your owners, not your aides. Same IdentoGO fee scale.
  • At revalidation, submit fingerprint appointment confirmation to avoid a 30-day termination gap.

⏳ Timeline

2002Direct-caregiver background check law (§ 44-7-2910) enacted (Act 242, eff. July 1, 2002).
2004Fingerprint requirement added for the federal/national check (Act 264, eff. July 6, 2004).
2022Medicaid FCBC (agency/owner) requirement for high-risk providers, effective Aug 15, 2022.

📁 "Do prints have to be physically in the employee folder?"

  • No. IdentoGO captures and transmits them; you never possess a print card.
  • The folder must contain the check result / DPH eligibility determination, retained as received (plus proof of when the check was initiated).

⚠️ Legacy aides (hired years ago, no prints) — sort the files

  • (a) State check done + residency documented → likely compliant; just document residency if missing.
  • (b) State check done, residency never verified → the federal check should have run.
  • (c) No check at all → clear violation.
  • Fix (b)/(c): run the missing checks NOW + write a dated corrective-action memo (never quietly backfill). A break ≥ 1 year = re-run the check before resuming.
  • Ramifications: missing checks = standard Reg 61-77 survey deficiency (citations + plan of correction); Medicaid overpayment exposure if aides serve Medicaid clients. Take the sort to a healthcare attorney before the next survey/revalidation.
