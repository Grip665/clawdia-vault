# CLTC Waiver Reference — HIV/AIDS & ADHC (SCDHHS)

> **Purpose:** One-stop reference for CLTC waiver policy questions, built from SCDHHS Phoenix **Help section** documents. Confirmed 2026-08-10. Use for case management decisions *and* the new admin job.
> **Source of truth:** Phoenix Help → `/policy` (Help Desk 803-898-2760; the yellow "Report a Problem" button for system issues).

---

## 0. The golden rules (know these first)

1. **One HCBS waiver at a time.** A member can be enrolled in a single Medicaid HCBS waiver. You cannot stack waivers (e.g., HIV/AIDS + Community Choices) to collect services from both.
2. **Waiver service vs State Plan service.** *Waiver services* are only available through a waiver (tied to waiver enrollment + level of care). *State Plan services* (medical, dental, vision, behavioral health, DME, therapy; e.g., in-home children's personal care, children's private duty nursing, incontinence supplies) are available to any eligible Medicaid member, including those on a waiver **waiting list**.
3. **ADHC = waiver service, not State Plan.** So it can only be delivered through a waiver that covers it.
4. **Case management authorization:** Case Manager authorizes waivered services based on assessed need + participant choice of provider (referrals to provider of choice).

## 1. The six HCBS waivers (SCDHHS Waiver Summary Chart, help #546)

### Community Choices (CC) — *the big one for adults*
- **Serves:** 18+, meets **Nursing Facility level of care**.
- **Services:** Case Management · Personal Care I/II · Attendant Care · Companion Care · Home Delivered Meals · Nutritional Supplements · **Adult Day Health Care** · **ADHC Transportation** · **ADHC Nursing** · Respite Care · Personal Emergency Response System · Tele-monitoring · Pest Control · Home Accessibility Adaptations · Specialized Medical Equipment & Supplies · Enhanced Pest Control.
- **Intake:** DHHS/CLTC Centralized Intake 888-971-1637; e-referral https://phoenix.scdhhs.gov/eref/

### HIV/AIDS (SC.0186)
- **Serves:** Medicaid eligible, any age, diagnosed with HIV/AIDS, meets **At-Risk of Hospitalization** level of care.
- **Services:** Case Management · Personal Care I/II · Attendant Care · Companion · Home Delivered Meals · Nutritional Supplements · Pest Control · Private Duty Nursing · Home Accessibility Adaptations · Specialized Medical Equipment & Supplies · Enhanced Pest Control.
- **Does NOT include ADHC.**
- Intake same as above (CLTC).

### Mechanical Ventilator Dependent (Vent)
- **Serves:** 21+, requires life-sustaining mechanical ventilation ≥6 hrs/day, NF level of care.
- **Services:** CM · Personal Care I/II · Attendant Care · Private Duty Nursing · Specialized Medical Equipment & Supplies · Respite · PERS · Home Accessibility Adaptations · Pest Control · Home Delivered Meals · Nutritional Supplements · Enhanced Pest Control.

### Community Supports (CS) — *DDSN-run*
- **Serves:** intellectual/related disability, all ages, **ICF/IID** level of care.
- **Services:** Personal Care I/II · **Adult Day Health Care** · **ADHC Nursing** · **ADHC Transportation** · Respite · Environmental Mods · Assistive Technology & Appliances (± assessment) · Incontinence Supplies · Private Vehicle Mods (± assessment) · Behavior Support · Day Activity · Career Prep · Community Services · Employment · Support Center · In-Home Support · PERS · Waiver CM.
- **Contact:** DDSN Single Point of Entry 1-800-289-7012.

### Intellectual Disability/Related Disabilities (ID/RD) — *DDSN-run*
- **Serves:** intellectual/related disability, all ages, **ICF/IID**.
- **Services:** Personal Care I/II · Residential Habilitation · Environmental Mods · Private Vehicle Mods (± assessment) · Specialized Medical Equipment & Assistive Tech (± assessment) · Incontinence Supplies · Respite · Audiology · Adult Companion · Nursing · Adult Dental · Adult Vision · **Adult Day Health Care** · **ADHC Nursing** · **ADHC Transportation** · Adult Attendant Care · Behavior Support · Career Prep · Employment · Day Activity · Community Services · Support Center.
- **Contact:** DDSN 1-800-289-7012.

### Head & Spinal Cord Injury (HASCI) — *DDSN-run*
- **Serves:** 0–65, head/spinal cord injury or similar, NF or ICF/IID LOC.
- **Services:** Career Prep · Day Activity · Employment · Attendant Care/Personal Assistance · Health & Peer Guidance (Participant Directed) · Residential Habilitation · Supplies/Equipment/AT · Incontinence Supplies · Respite · PERS · PT · OT · Psychological · Behavior Support · Nursing · Speech & Hearing · Private Vehicle Mods · Environmental Mods · AT Consultation · Pest Control · Waiver CM.
- **Contact:** DDSN 1-800-289-7012.

## 2. HIV/AIDS waiver deep-dive (Policy & Procedure Manual, help #618, Nov 1, 2022)

- **Full covered service set:** Attendant Care · Companion · Case Management · Personal Care I · Personal Care II · Home Delivered Meals · Home Accessibility Adaptations/Environmental Mods · Private Duty Nursing · Pest Control · Advanced Pest Control · Specialized Medical Equipment (Handheld Shower + Nutritional Supplements).
- **Initial Visit:** CM must complete an **initial visit within 30 days after enrollment** (per CC manual 05.31).
- **Service Plan:** uses CLTC Community Choices procedures (6.2). Service Authorization uses participant choice (CC 07.22).
- **Physician's Order required only for:** Medicaid Nursing Services and Nutritional Supplements.
- **CRCF (residential care facility) restriction:** a participant residing in a **CRCF** may receive only **Case Management + Nutritional Supplements** through the waiver. They may **NOT** receive PCI, PCII, PDN, Home Delivered Meals, Environmental Mods, or Attendant Care while in the CRCF.
- **Hospice:** a member can be on a waiver and Hospice at the same time, but services are limited (see Hospice section). Children on Medicaid hospice + HIV waiver need Central Office approval for CM.

## 3. ADHC (Adult Day Health Care) deep-dive (Scope doc, help #758)

- **Definition:** Restores/maintains/promotes health status via ambulatory health care + health-related supportive services delivered in an **ADHC center**. It is an **HCBS waiver** scope of service.
- **Unit of service:** participant day of ADHC; minimum **5 hours** (transportation time not counted). Care Plan + current Service Provision Form govern.
- **Staffing:** RN requirements scale with census (e.g., 1 RN for 1–44 participants; 1 RN + 1 for 45–88; more for 89–133; 134+ ratio continues).
- **Includes daily non-billable services (in the daily rate):** RN nursing (or supervised), coordination with physician/therapist/agencies, documentation, etc.
- **Other ADHC docs in the help section:** ADHC – Nursing Physician's Orders (July 2024); DHHS Form 122DC – ADHC Form (July 2024); ADHC Mode of Transportation (July 2022).
- **HCBS settings compliance:** ADHCs meeting Category 1/2/3 (facility-based, on institutional grounds, or isolating) must be assessed & certified waiver-compliant by SCDHHS before Medicaid reimbursement.

## 4. 🔥 THE KEY ISSUE — HIV/AIDS waiver + ADHC = CONFLICT

**Q:** Can someone on the HIV/AIDS waiver attend ADHC?
**A:** **No.** Confirmed via SCDHHS help docs (8/10/2026).

**Why (3 steps):**
1. ADHC is an **HCBS waiver** service (help #758) — not State Plan.
2. ADHC is **NOT** in the HIV/AIDS waiver's covered services (help #618 manual; help #546 summary chart confirms ADHC absent from HIV/AIDS column).
3. A member can only be enrolled in **one HCBS waiver** at a time.

**Which waivers DO cover ADHC?** Community Choices (incl. ADHC Transportation + ADHC Nursing), Community Supports, and ID/RD.

**Resolution for a client:**
- The HIV/AIDS waiver member **cannot receive ADHC** under that waiver.
- To get ADHC, they'd need to be on a waiver that covers it — typically **Community Choices** (requires 18+ and NF level of care) — which means **disenrolling from HIV/AIDS** (losing those services). You cannot hold both.
- So it's a genuine either/or: **stay HIV/AIDS (no ADHC)** vs **switch to CC (ADHC available)** — assuming the member meets CC eligibility.
- Not a global denial of ADHC — it's a waiver-enrollment conflict.
